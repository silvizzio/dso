A buyer who commits off-plan takes a delivery risk: the building may be late, may change, or may not be built at all. The best evidence against that risk is what the developer has already built.

## See what was delivered and when

| Year | Property | What it added |
|---|---|---|
| 2005 | DSO HQ | The headquarters of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority |
| 2015 | TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs |
| 2020 | LIU 8 | Light industrial units |

### What the record shows

- **Three types of building.** A headquarters, a campus of offices and labs, and light industrial units.
- **All still in use.** DSO has built each type and runs it today.
- **A direct line to District IO.** District IO combines the first two types at a larger scale, with homes and a hotel added.

## Read the development records

The right panel of the Archive view has two parts:

- **Buildings over time**: a chart that counts the featured buildings in each year.
- **Development records**: a list of what was completed in each year, with the most recent addition marked as new.

Read together with the archived imagery, they show both what DSO built and the district that grew around it.
