Imagery shows that a district has grown, but it does not say what was built, what type of building it was, or when it was finished. A careful buyer wants both the overall impression and the facts behind it.

Alongside the archive, the platform keeps a structured record of what was completed and when, so visitors can read DSO's track record as well as see it.

![the archive view: the record of completions sits in the right panel beside the imagery](/dso/api/img/05-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

## The record turns imagery into evidence

The record lists every completed property together with its year of completion. It puts a name to what the imagery only shows. With both in view, visitors can match each building on the map to the date DSO delivered it.

## How to read the record

1. Open the archive view in Past.
2. Look at the chart of buildings over time, which shows how quickly the district has grown.
3. Read through the development records. Each completion is listed with its year, and the most recent one is marked as New.
4. Go back to Future, now with good reason to believe in the offer.

## The record links DSO's past to District IO

The record covers the same types of property that District IO brings together on a larger scale: headquarters, campus offices and labs, and industrial space. Buyers can see that DSO has already built and operated every one of these.

This is the key connection the record makes for a buyer. The question is no longer whether DSO can deliver buildings like these. It is simply a matter of when District IO will be ready.

## The final step in the sales conversation

For buyers, delivery risk can now be judged on facts: what was built, what type of building it is, and how it is being used today.

For DSO, the record completes the case that Future began. The offer has been presented, tested against the present, and now tested against the past.

## Design decisions behind the record

- **The chart shows the pace, and the list names each delivery,** so visitors get both the overall trend and the specific facts.
- **The most recent delivery is highlighted,** because it is the strongest evidence of what DSO is capable of delivering today.
