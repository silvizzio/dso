Satellite imagery shows physical growth, but visitors also need to know which buildings they are looking at and when they were completed.
The delivery record supplies those details alongside the archive.

![The archive map with completion records in the right panel](/dso/api/img/05-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

## Matching buildings to their completion years

The record lists completed properties and their completion years.
Visitors can connect a named project with its location on the map and see how it fits into the district's development.
This adds detail that may be difficult to read from imagery alone.

## Reading the chart and records

Open the archive in Past.
The right panel contains a chart showing buildings over time, followed by development records with the year of each completion.
The most recent entry carries a New label to make it easier to find.

The chart gives an overview of the pace of growth.
The list provides the individual projects behind that pattern.
After reviewing them, visitors can return to Future to continue exploring District IO.

## Relating completed projects to District IO

The record includes headquarters, campus offices and labs, and industrial space.
These are relevant examples for visitors considering similar uses within District IO.
They show DSO's experience with completed properties that buyers can examine in the existing district.

That experience helps inform a discussion about the proposal.
Questions about District IO's own programme still need to be answered using its delivery information, which remains indicative until confirmed.

## Using the record in a property discussion

Visitors can refer to named projects, their building types and completion years when asking about DSO's development experience.
They can also switch to Now to examine how existing properties are used today.

For the commercial team, this connects the proposal in Future with examples from the present and the past.
The most recent completion is easy to identify, while the full record gives it historical context.
