The delivery record identifies the projects visible in the archive and gives their completion years. It helps visitors connect the district's physical growth with named buildings, rather than having to recognise them from satellite imagery alone.

![The archive map with completion records in the right panel](/dso/api/img/05-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

## Reading the record

Open **Past** and look at the right panel. A chart shows buildings over time, followed by individual development records with their completion years. In the 2020 example, LIU 8 carries a **New** label to identify the completion highlighted for that year.

The chart gives an overview of the pace of development; the records name the projects behind it. Read the dates alongside the map, particularly where later records remain visible as dimmed entries.

## Relating earlier projects to District IO

The featured completions include headquarters, campus offices and labs, and industrial space. Visitors considering similar uses in District IO can use these as examples of DSO's experience and switch to Now to examine the buildings as they are used today.

This gives the commercial team specific properties to refer to during a discussion about the proposal. Questions about District IO's own timing still need its programme, whose dates remain indicative until confirmed.
