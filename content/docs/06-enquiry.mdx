Every property, phase and building has an Enquire button. The form sends your contact details and the property you looked at to the DSO commercial team, so you do not have to explain it again.

![the enquiry form over the map, with the property figures at the top](/dso/api/img/06-future-lod3-district-io-1a-form.jpg)

## You can enquire from any level

| Level | Example | What the team receives |
|---|---|---|
| Property | TechnoHub 4 | The property and its current availability |
| Phase | District IO, Phase 1A | The phase and its figures |
| Sub-phase | Phase 1A-1 | The sub-phase and its buildings |
| Building | Techno Lab Block | The building and its design data |

## The form asks for five things

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| Full name | Yes |
| Company name | Yes |
| Email | Yes |
| Phone number | Yes |
| Message | No |

## Sending takes four steps

<StepList>
<Step number={1}>Select Enquire on a property, phase or building.</Step>
<Step number={2}>Enter your full name, company name, email and phone number.</Step>
<Step number={3}>Optional: tell the team what you are looking for.</Step>
<Step number={4}>Select Enquire. Your details go to the DSO commercial team.</Step>
</StepList>

To continue on your phone, scan the QR code in the left panel.
