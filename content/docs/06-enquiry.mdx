Every property has an Enquire button. The form carries the selected property, so the commercial team knows what the visitor looked at.

![the enquiry form over the map, with the property figures at the top](/dso/api/img/06-future-lod3-district-io-1a-form.jpg)

## Send an enquiry

<StepList>
<Step number={1}>Select Enquire on a property, a phase or a building.</Step>
<Step number={2}>Enter your full name, company name, email and phone number.</Step>
<Step number={3}>Optional: add a message about what you are looking for.</Step>
<Step number={4}>Select Enquire. Your details go to the DSO commercial team.</Step>
</StepList>

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| Full name | Yes |
| Company name | Yes |
| Email | Yes |
| Phone number | Yes |
| Message | No |
