Photos show a building at its best. Live cameras show it as it is. In Now, every built property has its CCTV cameras in the right panel, and the district has a video gallery.

## Open a live camera

1. Find the CCTV tiles in the right panel of a built property.
2. Select a tile to open the camera full size.

![a CCTV camera open full size, with the live status and camera details](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-cctv-enlarge.jpg)

The full view shows:

- the live image
- the camera name and zone
- the live status

## Watch the district videos

1. Select a video tile in the right panel.
2. Use the thumbnail strip under the player to move between videos.

![the video gallery open full size, with the thumbnail strip](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-video-enlarge.jpg)
