Photographs provide a useful introduction to a property, but they show one place at one moment.
The Digital Twin also includes camera feeds and videos to give visitors more context.
Cameras are intended to show current activity, while videos introduce the district and the people who use it.

## Opening a camera feed

A camera can show the entrance, lobby or other area it covers, including activity visible at that moment.
That view adds context to the property details, while remaining limited to the camera's position and field of view.

Look for camera tiles in the right panel of an existing building.
A connected feed is labelled as live.
Select a tile to open the camera full screen, with its name, location and live status.
The current screens use placeholder feeds until DSO confirms the camera list and access.

![A full-screen camera view with its name and live-status label](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-cctv-enlarge.jpg)

## Watching district videos

Videos show activity, events and people in the district, adding detail that the map cannot convey on its own.
They play without narration, which suits a large touch screen at an exhibition stand.

Select a video tile in the right panel to open the player.
Use the thumbnails below it to switch between videos.

![The full-screen video player with a thumbnail strip below](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-video-enlarge.jpg)

## Keeping the media in context

Cameras and videos serve different purposes, so their labels need to make that distinction clear.
A live-status label identifies a connected camera, while a produced video presents recorded material about the district.
Placeholder content must remain identified until it is replaced.

Both viewers open over the map and close back to the same location.
Visitors can watch the media, then continue exploring the property or send an enquiry without finding their place again.

<Callout type="note" title="Camera feeds are placeholders">
The camera content on these screens is placeholder material until DSO confirms its camera list and feed access.
See the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) for the outstanding requirements.
</Callout>
