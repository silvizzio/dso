Past shows the district as it was in an earlier year. It answers the question every off-plan buyer should ask: has this developer delivered before? The map switches to archived satellite imagery, and the panels show only what existed in the year you choose.

![the district in 2020: archived satellite imagery and the three buildings completed by that year](/dso/api/img/05-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

<PlatformDiagram focus="past" />

## Past at a glance

- **Three buildings delivered** between 2005 and 2020:
  - DSO HQ, 2005
  - TechnoHub 4, 2015
  - LIU 8, 2020
- **Real imagery.** The archived images are photographs of the site at the time, not renderings.
- **Five year steps**: 2010, 2015, 2020, 2025 and 2026.

## What you can do in Past

1. [Step Through the Archive](/dso/docs/15-step-through-the-archive)
   - Move the timeline back and watch the district fill in.
2. [Check the Delivery Record](/dso/docs/16-check-the-delivery-record)
   - See what DSO built and when, and what it means for District IO.

## What Past does not show

- **No live data.** CCTV, building data and leasing status do not exist for an earlier year.
- **No District IO.** No part of it existed before today.
