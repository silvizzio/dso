An off-plan buyer needs to consider whether a development will be completed as proposed and on time.
A developer's record of completed work is useful evidence when assessing that question.

Past presents DSO's development history through archived satellite imagery and completion records.
Visitors can select an earlier year to see the district at that time, then follow its growth through the available years.
Past is a LOD 3 view, with the timeline, map and completion records shown together.
The timelapse connects the archive years from 2005 onwards with the Now view in 2026.

## Examining the development history

The archive lets visitors compare views of the same district over time.
They can see roads, plots and buildings appear, while the completion record identifies the properties and their delivery years.

Together, the imagery and records give visitors something specific to examine when discussing DSO's experience.
The history becomes part of the property information available on screen.

## What this adds to an off-plan decision

For a buyer considering District IO, Past provides examples of what DSO has already completed.
It gives context for questions about the proposed development, although previous completions do not confirm a future delivery date.

For the commercial team, the archive makes that history accessible during a discussion.
They can refer to completed properties and their dates while visitors examine the same information.

## Exploring Past

1. [Step through the archive](/dso/docs/15-step-through-the-archive) to see how the district changed across the available years.
2. [Check the delivery record](/dso/docs/16-check-the-delivery-record) to identify completed properties and their completion years.

The archive shows the physical changes, while the record names the projects behind them.
Both views help visitors understand the district's development.

## Keeping the historical view consistent

Past uses satellite photographs captured at the time rather than renders of a reconstructed district.
The selected timeline year identifies the period being explored.
The completion list helps distinguish buildings already delivered by that year from later entries.
Some dates and labels in the prototype still need reconciliation, as noted in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).

Current camera feeds, building readings and leasing status do not appear in this view.
Keeping present-day information out of the archive avoids mixing different periods on the same screen.
