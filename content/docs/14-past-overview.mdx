Past shows how DSO has developed through archive satellite imagery and records of completed projects. Visitors can choose an earlier year, examine the district at that time and follow the changes across the available years.

The timeline, map and completion records appear together at **LOD 3**. A timelapse connects the archive from 2010 onwards with the Now view in 2026, showing the district's growth as a sequence.

## Looking at DSO's completed work

For someone considering an off-plan property, previous projects provide examples of the developer's experience. The imagery shows roads, plots and buildings appearing over time, while the completion records identify the properties and their delivery years.

Together, they give visitors and the commercial team specific projects to discuss when reviewing District IO. Past completions can inform that conversation, but they do not confirm the delivery dates of a new proposal.

## Exploring the archive

1. [Step through the archive](/dso/docs/15-step-through-the-archive) to compare the district across the available years or play the timelapse.
2. [Check the delivery record](/dso/docs/16-check-the-delivery-record) to identify the buildings and read their completion years.

Past uses satellite photographs captured at the time, rather than a reconstructed model of the district. Current camera feeds, building readings and leasing availability are excluded so they are not mistaken for historical information.

## Reading the dates carefully

The timeline identifies the year being explored. Completion records are intended to distinguish properties already delivered by that year from later entries, which may remain visible but dimmed.

Some dates and headings in the prototype do not yet agree. The [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) lists these inconsistencies so they can be checked against DSO's records before release.
