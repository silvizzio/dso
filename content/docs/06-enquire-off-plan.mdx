When something in District IO fits, you can send an enquiry from the screen you are on. An off-plan enquiry registers your interest before the building exists. It is not a commitment. It puts you in contact with the DSO commercial team, who can share terms, timing and availability as the project moves forward.

## Enquire from the level you are looking at

Every level in Future has an Enquire button. The form carries what you selected, so the team knows whether you asked about the whole complex or one floor of one building.

| Level | Example | What the team receives |
|---|---|---|
| Complex | District IO | The complex and its figures |
| Phase | Phase 1A | The phase and its figures |
| Sub-phase | Phase 1A-1 | The sub-phase and its buildings |
| Building | Techno Lab Block | The building and its design data |

## Fill in the form

![the enquiry form over the map, with the property figures at the top](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-form.jpg)

The form opens over the map and shows the figures of what you selected at the top. It asks for five things.

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| Full name | Yes |
| Company name | Yes |
| Email | Yes |
| Phone number | Yes |
| Message | No |

Use the message to say what you are looking for, for example the floor area you need, the use, or when you want to move in. Select Enquire to send. Your details go to the DSO commercial team. To continue on your phone, scan the QR code in the left panel.
