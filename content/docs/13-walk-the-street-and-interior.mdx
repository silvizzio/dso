A map shows where a building is. It does not show what it is like to arrive there, or to stand inside. Street view and the interior tour close that gap.

## Open street view

Street view answers the questions a map cannot:

- What does the approach look like?
- How far is the walk?
- What sits next door?

To open it:

1. Drag the pin from the map controls.
2. Drop it on a lit street. The lit streets are the routes with street view.

![dragging the pin: the walkable streets light up](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-drag-and-place-pin.jpg)

## Walk towards a building

When you drop the pin, the street view opens at eye level.

- **Drag** to look around.
- **Select the arrow** to walk towards the next building.
- **Check the mini map** in the corner to see where you are and which way you face.
- **Enquire** stays at the bottom of the screen the whole time.

![street view after the pin drop, walking towards TechnoHub 4](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-placed-pin-exterior-virtual-tour.jpg)

## Step inside an available space

1. Select **View interior** in the right panel of an available property.
2. Drag to look around.
3. Select the arrow to move to the next point.

![the interior tour on a vacant office floor](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-virtual-tour-interior.jpg)

While you look around, check what a floor plan cannot show:

- the windows and the view
- the ceiling height
- how much daylight the floor gets
