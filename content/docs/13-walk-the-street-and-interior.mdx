A map shows where a building is, but an eye-level view helps explain what it is like to approach it and move through the space.
Visitors at an exhibition stand can use street view and interior tours to take that closer look.

Street view covers the area outside a building.
The interior tour lets visitors explore space offered for lease and decide whether they want to arrange an on-site viewing.

## What visitors can look for

Street view helps visitors examine the approach, the walk from the road and neighbouring buildings.
Inside, they can look at windows, ceiling height and the impression of natural light in the tour.
These views add context to the map and floor plans when comparing properties.

## Using street view

Drag the pin from the map controls.
Streets with street-view coverage light up as you move it.

![Dragging the pin highlights streets with street-view coverage](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod2-drag-and-place-pin.jpg)

Drop the pin on a highlighted street to open the eye-level view at that location.
Drag to look around, then tap an arrow to move to the next point.
The mini map shows your position and the direction you are facing, helping you stay oriented as you move.

![The exterior tour after placing the pin, with the location retained on the mini map](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-placed-pin-exterior-virtual-tour.jpg)

<DocVideo src="/dso/videos/docs/04-now-lod3-street-view.mp4" caption="Moving along the street towards a building at eye level" />

Highlighting the available streets shows visitors where they can place the pin before they release it.

## Taking an interior tour

Select View interior from a property that offers the action.
Drag to look around and tap an arrow to move between viewpoints.

<DocVideo src="/dso/videos/docs/04-now-lod3-virtual-tour-interior.mp4" caption="An example interior tour through a shopping mall" />

The tour helps visitors examine features that can be difficult to judge from a floor plan, including windows, views and ceiling height.
The [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) lists DSO's interior renders among the materials still needed for the virtual tour.

## Preparing for an on-site visit

A virtual tour helps visitors decide which spaces they want to investigate further.
They can bring specific questions to the commercial team and use an on-site viewing to examine those details in person.

The exterior tour shown here keeps Enquire visible.
For other views, return to a proposed or available property if you need to open its enquiry form.
