Street view and interior tours let visitors look around a property at eye level before arranging an on-site visit. Street view shows the approach and neighbouring buildings; an interior tour gives an impression of the space inside, including its windows, ceiling height and natural light.

At an exhibition stand or sales centre, these tools help visitors investigate details that may be difficult to judge from the map or a floor plan alone.

## Opening street view

Drag the pin from the map controls. Streets with coverage light up as you move it, showing where you can place it.

![Dragging the pin highlights streets with street-view coverage](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod2-drag-and-place-pin.jpg)

Drop the pin on a highlighted street to open the view at that location. Drag to look around and tap an arrow to move to the next viewpoint. The mini map shows where you are and the direction you are facing.

![The exterior tour after placing the pin, with the location retained on the mini map](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-placed-pin-exterior-virtual-tour.jpg)

<DocVideo src="/dso/videos/docs/04-now-lod3-street-view.mp4" caption="Moving along the street towards a building at eye level" />

Use this view to examine the approach to the building, its surroundings and the route from the road.

## Looking inside

Select **View interior** from a property that offers it. As in street view, drag to look around and tap an arrow to move between viewpoints.

![An example office interior tour, with a movement arrow and Enquire button](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-virtual-tour-interior.jpg)

<DocVideo src="/dso/videos/docs/04-now-lod3-virtual-tour-interior.mp4" caption="An example interior tour through a shopping mall" />

The screenshot shows an example office interior, while the video demonstrates movement through a shopping mall. They illustrate the tour experience; DSO's interior renders are still among the materials required in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).

## Following up after a tour

A tour can help you decide which spaces to investigate further and what to ask during an on-site viewing. It is useful for forming an impression, while details such as dimensions and suitability still need checking for the property you are considering.

The exterior and office-interior examples both keep **Enquire** visible. In other views, return to a proposed or available property to open its enquiry form.
