section: Future

From a phase you can go down to a single building and its floors. This is where you judge fit: how big a floor is, what each floor is for, and where the lab floors, the retail and the plant sit. The kiosk takes you there in three taps, and each step keeps the one before it in view, so you always know where you are.

<StepList>
<Step number={1}>Select a **phase** to see its sub-phases.</Step>
<Step number={2}>Select a **sub-phase** to see its buildings.</Step>
<Step number={3}>Select a **building** to see its levels, floor area and use mix.</Step>
</StepList>

## Open a phase to see its sub-phases

![Phase 1A: sub-phases 1A-1, 1A-2 and 1A-3 on the map, and the phase figures on the right](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a.jpg)

Sub-phases group the buildings of a phase by what they contain. In Phase 1A, two sub-phases hold the office clusters and the third holds the homes and open space.

| Sub-phase | What it contains |
|---|---|
| 1A-1 | Office and techno lab cluster |
| 1A-2 | Second office cluster |
| 1A-3 | Residential blocks and open space |

## Open a sub-phase to see its buildings

![Phase 1A-1: Techno Lab Block and Office Tower A on the map](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-1.jpg)

The selected sub-phase has a glowing outline on the map, and each building in it has its own marker with its use and height. The left panel lists the same buildings, so you can select from the map or from the list.

## Open a building to see its floors

![Techno Lab Block: levels, building GFA, typical floor plate, use mix, and floor by floor areas](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-building-level.jpg)

This is the level a tenant uses to judge fit. The typical floor plate is the area of one standard floor, and it tells you how much space you get if you take a whole floor. The use mix shows how the building splits between uses, and the floor by floor list shows what each floor is for.

| Item | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Levels | Number of floors, for example G+5 |
| Building GFA | Total floor area of the building |
| Typical plate | Floor area of a typical floor |
| Use mix | Share of floor area by use |
| Floor by floor | Use and area of each floor |

<Callout type="note" title="Indicative data">
Sub-phase and building data are indicative. The concept report gives areas by phase, not by building.
</Callout>
