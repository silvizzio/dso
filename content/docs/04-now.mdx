District IO is the offer. A visitor also asks two more questions: what can I use today, and how well do the existing buildings run? The built stock answers both.

Now shows what exists today and what you can lease now. TechnoHub 4 has space available, with live building data and an interior tour. DSO HQ and LIU 8 are fully leased. District IO is not built, so it sits under Future until construction starts.

## Three properties exist today

| Property | Type | Status | What you can do |
|---|---|---|---|
| TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs | Available | See each suite, view interior, enquire |
| DSO HQ | Headquarters | Leased | See the building and its spaces, enquire for future availability |
| LIU 8 | Light industrial | Leased | See the building, enquire for future availability |

Each property plays a different part in the story. TechnoHub 4 is the one you can act on today. DSO HQ and LIU 8 are proof: buildings that are full and running.

## The district overview shows the whole portfolio

![Now overview: the areas of interest with their status, and the district summary on the right](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-overview.jpg)

The overview is the starting point for Now. The left panel lists the areas with their status, so you see at once where space is available. The right panel shows a chart of buildings over time, which counts how many of the featured buildings existed in each year, and the district video gallery.

## Available properties list every space you can lease

![TechnoHub 4: each suite and lab with its size and status, and the key figures on the right](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4.jpg)

TechnoHub 4 is a campus of offices and labs, completed in 2015. The left panel lists each space you can lease with its area and status. The right panel gives the figures a tenant compares first: how many suites are free, their size range, the rent, how full the building is, and the parking.

The kiosk quotes rent per square metre per year. To estimate the yearly rent of a space, multiply its area by the rate. For example, at AED 1,150 per m² per year, a 240 m² suite costs about AED 276,000 a year before service charges. The rates and availability on screen are indicative until DSO connects its leasing data.

A space marked **Under offer** has an offer in progress. It is not free yet, but it is not taken either, so it is still worth an enquiry.

| Right panel | Suites available, area range, rent from, occupancy, parking, completion year, CCTV |

## Building level shows how the building performs

Building Info opens data from the building management system (BMS), the software that runs cooling, lighting and power. For a tenant, it answers questions a brochure cannot: is the building comfortable, is it well maintained, and what will it cost to run?

![TechnoHub 4 building level: system status, comfort, and occupancy, temperature and energy load by floor](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4-building-level.jpg)

| Item | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Systems online | How many building systems report data. A high figure means the building is monitored and maintained. |
| Zone comfort | How many zones are in the comfort range for temperature and air quality |
| Retail, set point, load now | Retail space let, the temperature the cooling aims for, and the current power draw |
| By level | Occupancy, temperature and energy load for each floor |
| Energy today | Energy use today against a typical day |

Each bar in the floor charts is one floor, from the ground floor (G) at the left to the top floor at the right. Read the floors against each other rather than against a target. A floor that is much warmer, or uses much more energy than the floors around it, is worth asking about.

## Media lets you see the property before a visit

Photos show a building at its best. The media in Now show it as it is: live cameras, videos of the district, street view and a walk through the inside.

### CCTV shows the building live

Select a CCTV tile to open the camera full size. It shows the entrance, lobbies and common areas as they are right now.

![a CCTV camera open full size, with the live status and camera details](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-cctv-enlarge.jpg)

### Videos tell the district story

Select a video tile to open the gallery. The thumbnail strip moves between videos.

![the video gallery open full size, with the thumbnail strip](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-video-enlarge.jpg)

### Street view puts you on the street

Street view answers the questions a map cannot: what the approach looks like, how far the walk is, and what sits next door. Drag the pin from the map controls and drop it on a lit street. The lit streets are the routes with street view.

![dragging the pin: the walkable streets light up](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-drag-and-place-pin.jpg)

When you drop the pin, the street view opens at eye level. Drag to look around. Select the arrow to walk towards the next building. The mini map in the corner shows where you are and which way you face.

![street view after the pin drop, walking towards TechnoHub 4](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-placed-pin-exterior-virtual-tour.jpg)

### The interior tour takes you inside

View interior opens a 360 degree tour of an available space. You see the real floor: its windows, its ceiling height and how much light it gets. Drag to look around. Select the arrow to move to the next point.

![the interior tour on a vacant office floor](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-virtual-tour-interior.jpg)
