Now shows what exists today and what you can lease now. TechnoHub 4 has space available. DSO HQ and LIU 8 are fully leased. District IO is not built, so it is under Future.

## Three properties exist today

| Property | Type | Status | What you can do |
|---|---|---|---|
| TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs | Available | See each suite, view interior, enquire |
| DSO HQ | Headquarters | Leased | See the building and its spaces, enquire for future availability |
| LIU 8 | Light industrial | Leased | See the building, enquire for future availability |

## Building level shows how the building performs

Building Info opens the live data from the building management system.

![TechnoHub 4 building level: system status, comfort, and occupancy, temperature and energy load by floor](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-available-technohub-4-building-level.jpg)

| Item | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Systems online | How many building systems report data |
| Zone comfort | How many zones are in the comfort range |
| Retail, set point, load now | Retail space let, temperature set point, current energy load |
| By level | Occupancy, temperature and energy load for each floor |
| Energy today | Energy use today against a typical day |

## Media lets you see the property before a visit

### CCTV shows the building live

Select a CCTV tile to open the camera full size.

![a CCTV camera open full size, with the live status and camera details](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-cctv-enlarge.jpg)

### Videos tell the district story

Select a video tile to open the gallery. The thumbnail strip moves between videos.

![the video gallery open full size, with the thumbnail strip](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-video-enlarge.jpg)

### Street view puts you on the street

Drag the pin from the map controls and drop it on a lit street. The lit streets are the routes with street view.

![dragging the pin: the walkable streets light up](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-drag-and-place-pin.jpg)

When you drop the pin, the street view opens. Drag to look around. Select the arrow to walk towards the next building. The mini map shows where you are.

![street view after the pin drop, walking towards TechnoHub 4](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-placed-pin-exterior-virtual-tour.jpg)

### The interior tour takes you inside

View interior opens a 360 degree tour. Drag to look around. Select the arrow to move to the next point.

![the interior tour on a vacant office floor](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-virtual-tour-interior.jpg)
