Past shows the district in an earlier year. Use the year steps to move between 2010, 2015, 2020, 2025 and 2026.

![the district in 2020: archived satellite imagery and the three buildings completed by that year](/dso/api/img/04-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

## What changes in Past

| Region | Content |
|---|---|
| Map | Archived satellite imagery for the selected year. |
| Left panel | Only the areas that existed in that year, each with its completion year. District IO is not shown. |
| Right panel | The Archive view: a description, a chart of buildings over time, and the development records by year. |

Past has no CCTV and no live data, because these do not exist for an earlier year.
