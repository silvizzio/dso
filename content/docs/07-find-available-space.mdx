Now shows the district as it is today. Its first job is to show you where you can lease space right now. The overview marks each property with its status, and an available property lists every space you can take, with its size, status and rent.

![Now overview: the areas of interest with their status, and the district summary on the right](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-overview.jpg)

## See which properties have space

Now shows only buildings that exist. District IO is not built, so it sits under Future until construction starts.

| Property | Type | Status | What you can do |
|---|---|---|---|
| TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs | Available | See each suite, view interior, enquire |
| DSO HQ | Headquarters | Leased | See the building and its spaces, enquire for future availability |
| LIU 8 | Light industrial | Leased | See the building, enquire for future availability |

The left panel lists the areas with their status, so you see at once where space is available. The right panel shows a chart of buildings over time and the district video gallery.

## Compare the spaces in an available property

![TechnoHub 4: each suite and lab with its size and status, and the key figures on the right](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4.jpg)

TechnoHub 4 is a campus of offices and labs, completed in 2015. The left panel lists each space you can lease with its area and status. The right panel gives the figures a tenant compares first: how many suites are free, their size range, the rent, how full the building is, and the parking.

| Region | Content |
|---|---|
| Left panel | The building history, then each suite or lab with its area and status |
| Right panel | Suites available, area range, rent from, occupancy, parking, completion year, CCTV |
| Actions | Enquire, Building Info, View interior |

A space marked **Under offer** has an offer in progress. It is not free yet, but it is not taken either, so it is still worth an enquiry.

## Read the rent

The kiosk quotes rent per square metre per year. To estimate the yearly rent of a space, multiply its area by the rate. For example, at AED 1,150 per m² per year, a 240 m² suite costs about AED 276,000 a year before service charges.

The rates and availability on screen are indicative until DSO connects its leasing data. Use them to compare spaces, and confirm the figures through an enquiry.
