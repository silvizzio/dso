An available property lists every space you can lease today, with its size, status and rent. Use it to shortlist spaces, then check the building and the interior before you enquire.

## Compare the spaces in an available property

![TechnoHub 4: each suite and lab with its size and status, and the key figures on the right](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4.jpg)

TechnoHub 4 is a campus of offices and labs, completed in 2015.

- **The left panel** lists each space:
  - the building history at the top
  - each suite or lab, with its area and status
- **The right panel** gives the figures a tenant compares first:
  - how many suites are free, and their size range
  - the rent
  - how full the building is
  - parking and completion year
  - the building's CCTV
- **The actions** at the bottom: Enquire, Building Info and View interior.

### What Under offer means

A space marked **Under offer** has an offer in progress. It is not free yet, but it is not taken either, so it is still worth an enquiry.

## Read the rent

The kiosk quotes rent per square metre per year. To estimate the yearly rent of a space:

1. Find the rate, for example AED 1,150 per m² per year.
2. Multiply it by the area of the space.
   - A 240 m² suite at AED 1,150 costs about AED 276,000 a year.
3. Add service charges, which the rate does not include.

The rates and availability on screen are indicative until DSO connects its leasing data. Use them to compare spaces, and confirm the figures through an enquiry.
