Some visitors need space soon and cannot wait for District IO.
For them, the fastest path is whatever is free today.
Finding it usually means calls, emails and site visits before a shortlist exists.

The platform shortens that path.
Every property shows its status on the map, and an available property opens into a list of every space that can be leased.
A visitor can build a shortlist, and test it, in one visit.

![an available property opened: every space that can be leased, with its size and status](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4.jpg)

## The platform works as a live availability board

In the Now overview, each property carries a status label.
A visitor scanning the map sees at once which properties have space and which are full.
The search narrows before the visitor opens anything.

Opening an available property turns the left panel into its list of spaces.
Each space shows its size and status.
The right panel shows the terms and the facts about the building that a tenant compares first.

## Building and testing a shortlist takes five steps

1. Scan the status labels in the Now overview.
   Available properties stand out.
2. Open an available property.
   The left panel lists its spaces.
3. Compare the units by size and status, and read the terms in the right panel.
4. Test the shortlist from the same screen.
   - Open the building's live data to see how it performs.
   - Open the interior tour to see the unit itself.
5. Enquire about a specific space, with the property already attached.

The tests sit one tap away from the list on purpose.
A shortlist that has been checked against live data and a tour is a stronger basis for an enquiry than a list of sizes.

## Under offer keeps near-misses in play

A space marked **under offer** has an offer in progress but no signed lease.
The platform shows it, rather than hiding it, for two reasons.

- It tells the visitor that demand exists, which supports the case for the building.
- It keeps that unit open to enquiry.
  If the offer does not complete, the commercial team already has the next interested party.

## The availability board shortens the leasing cycle

For a tenant, the path from "is anything free?" to a tested shortlist takes minutes, without contacting anyone first.

For the commercial team, a visitor who shortlists at the platform arrives at the first conversation with specific spaces in mind.
The cycle from first contact to viewing gets shorter.
