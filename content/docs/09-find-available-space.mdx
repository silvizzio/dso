Visitors looking to move in the next few months can use Now to explore existing space while District IO is still being planned. The map brings property status, unit sizes and leasing details together so they can identify options before arranging a call or site visit.

The information currently shown is indicative. DSO still needs to connect its leasing data before visitors can rely on the availability and terms.

![An available property, with unit sizes and status labels in the left panel](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4.jpg)

## Comparing properties and units

Start with a property marked **Available** in the Now overview. Opening it brings up a list of spaces on the left, with a size and status for each one. Read these alongside the leasing terms and building details in the right panel.

If a space looks suitable, use **Building Info** to examine the building data or **View interior** to look around. The floor area tells you how much space is listed; the tour helps you judge its layout and appearance. Select **Enquire** when you are ready to discuss the property. The form is intended to carry the property details into the enquiry once submission is connected.

## What Under offer means

A space marked **Under offer** has an offer in progress, but the lease has not yet been signed. It remains visible so visitors can distinguish it from both available space and a completed lease.

If that space interests you, ask the commercial team about registering interest in case the offer does not proceed. Its status should be confirmed before you plan around it.

## Preparing for a viewing

Use the unit list and tours to identify the spaces you want to discuss, then note any questions about size, use or timing. This gives the commercial team a practical starting point for checking availability and arranging a relevant viewing.
