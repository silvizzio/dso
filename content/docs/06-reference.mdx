## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| AOI | Area of interest. One of the four areas the kiosk features. |
| LOD | Level of detail. One of the three zoom levels of the map. |
| Phase | A delivery stage of District IO (1A, 1B, 1C, 1D, 2). |
| Sub-phase | A part of a phase, for example 1A-1. |
| G+n | Ground floor plus n floors. G+5 is six storeys. |
| Completion | The date construction finishes. |
| Handover | The date a tenant or buyer gets the keys. Usually after completion. |
| Off-plan | A sale or lease before the building is complete. |
| Under offer | Space with an offer in progress. |

## Area measures

| Measure | Definition | District IO |
|---|---|---|
| Plot area | Land area of the site | 164,420 m² |
| GFA | Gross floor area. Total floor area inside the building walls. | 595,300 m² |
| GLA | Gross leasable area. Floor area a tenant can lease. | 429,000 m² |
| BUA | Built-up area. All built floor area, including parking and services. | 991,689 m² |
