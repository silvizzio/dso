This chapter explains the terms, measurements and data sources used throughout the platform.
Buyers can use it to interpret the figures, while the DSO team can check what remains outstanding before the November opening.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AOI | Area of interest. One of the four properties featured on the platform. |
| LOD | Level of detail. One of the map's three zoom levels. |
| Phase | A section of District IO (1A, 1B, 1C, 1D or 2) designed, built and handed over as one package. |
| Sub-phase | A part of a phase, such as 1A-1. |
| G+n | Ground floor plus n additional floors. For example, G+5 means six storeys in total. |
| Concept design | The first formal design stage, establishing the uses, phases and approximate building shapes. |
| Completion | The date construction finishes. |
| Handover | The date a buyer or tenant receives the keys, usually after completion. |
| Off-plan | A sale or lease agreed before a building is complete. |
| Under offer | Space with an offer in progress but no signed lease. |
| Floor plate | The area of a standard floor in a building. |
| BMS | Building management system. The software that controls cooling, lighting and power. |

## Understanding area measures

Different area measures describe different parts of a property.
A quoted area of 1,000 m² does not always mean 1,000 m² available for a tenant to use.
Check which measure accompanies a figure before comparing properties.

![The district figures at LOD 2, showing GFA alongside homes and hotel keys](/dso/api/img/02-lod2-dso-1.jpg)

| Measure | Meaning | District IO |
|---|---|---|
| Plot area | The land area of the site | 164,420 m² |
| GFA | Gross floor area. The total floor area inside the building walls. | 595,300 m² |
| GLA | Gross leasable area. The floor area a tenant can lease. | 429,000 m² |
| BUA | Built-up area. All built floor area, including parking and building services. | 991,689 m² |

For District IO, GLA is about 72% of GFA.
The remaining share includes areas such as lobbies, cores, corridors and plant rooms.
BUA is about 1.7 times GFA because it also includes parking and building services.
These figures describe the concept design, rather than completed space.

## Checking a figure's status

Each status label explains where the information stands and whether it needs to be replaced.
A confirmed source for a design figure does not make it a measurement of a completed building.

| Status | Meaning | What needs to happen |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | Taken from a DSO document | Retain the source and check for revisions when the design changes |
| Indicative | A realistic value used pending the required data | Replace when DSO provides or connects the data |
| Placeholder | Material from another source used to develop the layout | Replace before public use |

## Reading prototype dates and labels

Use the timeline selection to identify the year being explored, and treat the screenshots as interface examples.
Some labels still disagree: the 2005 archive screen has a different year in its right-hand heading, the DSO HQ view shows 2005 in its history and 2006 beside Completed, and the sample enquiry is headed DSO HQ while a District IO phase is open behind it.
These details need to be reconciled with the project records and selected property before release.
The guide does not use those conflicting labels to establish a completion date or confirm that enquiry routing works.
