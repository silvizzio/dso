Use this chapter to check the terms and measurements in the guide, find the source of a figure or see what still needs confirmation. The final table sets out the information and decisions required from DIEZ or DSO before the November opening.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AOI | Area of interest: one of the four properties featured on the platform. |
| LOD | Level of detail: one of the map's three zoom levels. |
| Phase | A section of District IO, such as 1A or 2, planned to be designed, built and handed over as one package. |
| Sub-phase | A part of a phase, such as 1A-1. |
| G+n | Ground floor plus n additional floors. G+5, for example, means six storeys in total. |
| Planned | A proposed property or phase. The year beside it is a target, not a record of completion. |
| Target year | The proposed year for a property or phase, subject to confirmation of the programme. |
| Concept design | The first formal design stage, establishing the uses, phases and approximate building shapes. |
| Completion | The point when construction finishes. |
| Handover | The point when the buyer or tenant receives the keys, usually after completion. |
| Off-plan | A sale or lease agreed before a building is complete. |
| Under offer | Space with an offer in progress but no signed lease. |
| Floor plate | The area of a standard floor in a building. |
| BMS | Building management system: software used to control systems such as cooling, lighting and power. |

## Comparing area figures

An area of 1,000 m² does not necessarily mean 1,000 m² that a tenant can rent. Different measurements include different parts of a property, so check whether a figure refers to land, total floor area or leasable space before comparing it with another.

![The district figures at LOD 2, showing GFA alongside homes and hotel keys](/dso/api/img/02-lod2-dso-1.jpg)

| Measure | What it includes | District IO |
|---|---|---|
| Plot area | The land occupied by the site | 164,420 m² |
| GFA: gross floor area | Total floor area inside the building walls | 595,300 m² |
| GLA: gross leasable area | Floor area available to lease | 429,000 m² |
| BUA: built-up area | All built floor area, including parking and building services | 991,689 m² |

District IO's GLA is about 72% of its GFA. The remaining area includes spaces such as lobbies, cores, corridors and plant rooms. BUA is about 1.7 times GFA because it also includes parking and building services. These are concept design figures, not measurements of completed buildings.

## Understanding the data status

The status identifies how a figure or piece of content is being used in the prototype. A confirmed source means the information comes from a DSO document; it does not turn a design figure into an as-built measurement.

| Status | Meaning | Required follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | Taken from a DSO document | Keep the source and check for revisions when the design changes |
| Indicative | An example value used until the required data is available | Replace it when DSO provides or connects the data |
| Placeholder | Material from another source used to develop the screen layout | Replace it before public use |

## Prototype labels that need correction

The screenshots illustrate the interface, but a few labels still conflict with the year or property selected:

- The 2005 archive screen has a different year in the right-hand heading.
- DSO HQ shows 2005 in its history and 2006 beside Completed.
- The sample enquiry form is headed DSO HQ while a District IO phase is open behind it.

Check these against the project records and selected property before release. The conflicting labels cannot establish a completion date, and the sample form does not confirm that submissions reach the commercial team.
