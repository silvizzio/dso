This chapter brings together the terms, measures and data sources behind the platform. Buyers can use it to make sense of the figures they see, and DSO teams can use it to check which figures are final and what is still needed before the November opening.

## Glossary

The platform and this guide use a small set of property and design terms. Each one is explained below.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| AOI | Area of interest. One of the four properties featured on the platform. |
| LOD | Level of detail. One of the three zoom levels of the map. |
| Phase | A delivery stage of District IO (1A, 1B, 1C, 1D, 2) that is designed, built and handed over as a single package. |
| Sub-phase | A part of a phase, for example 1A-1. |
| G+n | Ground floor plus n floors. For example, G+5 means six storeys. |
| Concept design | The first formal design stage, which sets the mix of uses, the phases and the approximate building shapes. |
| Completion | The date construction is finished. |
| Handover | The date a tenant or buyer receives the keys. This usually follows completion. |
| Off-plan | A sale or lease agreed before the building is complete. |
| Under offer | Space with an offer in progress but no signed lease. |
| Floor plate | The floor area of one standard floor of a building. |
| BMS | Building management system. The software that controls a building's cooling, lighting and power. |

## Area measures

Property figures use several different area measures, and they cannot be used interchangeably. A "1,000 m² office" can mean quite different amounts of usable space depending on which measure is being used. The table below explains each measure and gives its value for District IO.

![the district figures at LOD 2, which use GFA for floor area alongside homes and hotel keys](/dso/api/img/02-lod2-dso-1.jpg)

| Measure | Definition | District IO |
|---|---|---|
| Plot area | Land area of the site | 164,420 m² |
| GFA | Gross floor area. The total floor area inside the building walls. | 595,300 m² |
| GLA | Gross leasable area. The floor area a tenant can lease. | 429,000 m² |
| BUA | Built-up area. All built floor area, including parking and building services. | 991,689 m² |

Two ratios make these figures easier to understand. GLA is 72% of GFA, which means roughly a quarter of the floor area is taken up by lobbies, cores, corridors and plant rooms. BUA is about 1.7 times GFA, because it also includes parking and building services.

## Figure status

Not every figure on the platform is final yet. Each one is given a status, so that no one mistakes a placeholder for a confirmed fact.

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | From a DSO document | None |
| Indicative | A realistic placeholder value | Replace once DSO connects the data |
| Placeholder | Content from another source, used for layout only | Replace before any public use |
