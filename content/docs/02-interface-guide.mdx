A kiosk visitor has no training and little time. They walk up, touch the screen, and must find what they want in seconds. The screen still has to hold a whole district, with its buildings, figures, media and forms.

The screen stays simple by following three rules:

1. **The map is always the main object.** It fills the screen, and the panels sit at the edges.
2. **You choose on the left and read on the right.** Selecting something on the left or on the map updates the right panel.
3. **Detail grows as you zoom in.** The first view is calm, and the closest view is complete.

## The map has three levels of detail

The kiosk uses zoom levels to control how much it tells you at once. LOD stands for level of detail, a term from 3D mapping, where a model shows more detail the closer you get.

| Level | What you see | Panels |
|---|---|---|
| LOD 1 | The whole district from above | District figures along the bottom |
| LOD 2 | The district core around District IO | District figures along the bottom |
| LOD 3 | A tilted 3D view of the selected area | Left panel, right panel and map controls |

## LOD 1 and LOD 2 show the district at a glance

At the two wide levels there are no side panels. The area markers sit on the map, and the district figures sit in one band along the bottom.

![LOD 1: homes planned, available units, EV chargers, solar output, transport stops, cycling track and parking](/dso/api/img/02-lod1.jpg)

The figures answer the first questions most visitors have about a district:

- **Can people live here?**
  - Homes planned: the homes in District IO, from the concept report.
- **Can I find space?**
  - Available units: units you can lease today across the built properties.
- **How do I get here?**
  - Transport stops: bus stops in the district.
  - Cycling track: length of the jogging and bicycle track.
  - EV chargers: public charging points in the district.
- **Where do I park?**
  - Parking: parking spaces in the district.
- **How green is it?**
  - Solar now: current output of the district solar panels.

Each figure has a row of small icons under it, so the band reads from a few metres away. Some figures come from DSO drawings, and some are indicative until DSO connects its data. The [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) shows the status of each one.

![LOD 2: the district core, with District IO at the centre](/dso/api/img/02-lod2-dso-1.jpg)

LOD 2 moves closer to the district core, where District IO sits between DSO HQ and the lake. It is the bridge between the overview and the full interface.

## LOD 3 has five regions

At the closest level the full interface opens.

![the full LOD 3 layout: left panel, map, map controls and right panel](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-overview.jpg)

| Region | What it does |
|---|---|
| Header | DSO logo, compass heading, environment readings, date and time, user menu |
| Left panel | Controls what the map shows |
| Map | The 3D district. Markers show areas of interest. The selected building has a glowing outline. |
| Map controls | Timeline shortcut, basemap switch, drop pin, zoom |
| Right panel | Explains what you selected, and holds the actions |

The header readings are wind, sea level, air temperature and air quality.

## The left panel controls what you see

### Exploration mode

Each mode changes what the kiosk puts first, for a different kind of visitor:

- **Free** shows everything.
- **Business** puts investment and leasing first.
- **Research** puts labs and innovation sites first.

### Timeline

The timeline sets which version of the district you see:

- **Future**, the default: District IO and its phases.
- **Now**: the built district today.
- **Past**: the district in an earlier year.

Use the arrows or the year steps to move between years.

### Areas of interest

Select an area and the map flies to it, and the right panel shows its details. When you open an area, the left panel changes to the list of items inside it, with a Back link at the top. This is how you go from District IO to a phase, and from a phase to a building.

### Assets and Innovations

These are map filters. Turn one on to see where that type of place is in the district, and turn it off to clear the map.

- **Assets**: office, residential, commercial, education, retail, mobility, industrial and utilities.
- **Innovations**: innovation sites, for example labs.

## The right panel explains what you selected

The right panel always follows the same order, top to bottom. A visitor who has read one right panel can read them all.

1. Status label and title
2. Image gallery
3. Description
4. Key figures
5. Media: videos, and CCTV in Now
6. Actions: Enquire, Building Info, View interior

### The Enquire form

Enquire opens a short form. It carries the property you selected, so the DSO commercial team knows what you looked at. It asks for:

- Full name
- Company name
- Email
- Phone number
- Message (optional)

To continue on your phone, scan the QR code in the left panel.

## Map controls change the view

- **Timeline shortcut** opens the timeline.
- **Basemap switch** changes between satellite and street map.
- **Drop pin** opens street view: drag it to any lit street, and the view drops to eye level at that point.
- **Zoom** moves in and out.

## Status labels tell you what you can act on

Every area and space carries one label. It tells you two things at once: whether the space exists, and whether you can take it.

| Label | Meaning | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Available | Space you can lease now | Enquire, view interior |
| Under offer | An offer is in progress | Enquire to join the waiting list |
| Leased | No space is available now | Enquire for future availability |
| Coming 2030 | Not built. Construction has not started. | Enquire off-plan |
| Completed | Shown in Past. The year the building was completed. | None |
