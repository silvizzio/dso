The Digital Twin is designed for visitors using a touch screen at an exhibition stand or sales centre. The map stays at the centre of the experience: visitors can tap a property or choose it from a list, then read its details without losing sight of where it is.

The screen starts with a broad introduction to the district. As visitors zoom in or open a property, the panels reveal the information needed to explore it further.

## The three zoom levels

### Level 1: the whole district

![Level 1: the whole district with a strip of headline figures](/dso/api/img/02-lod1.jpg)

The opening screen shows the whole district, with headline figures and small icons along the bottom. There are no side panels at this level, so someone approaching the screen can get a quick introduction before choosing what to explore.

### Level 2: the district core

![Level 2: the district core, with District IO at the centre](/dso/api/img/02-lod2-dso-1.jpg)

The second level brings District IO and the surrounding buildings closer. It shows where the proposed complex sits within DSO, allowing visitors to get their bearings before opening a property's details.

### Level 3: properties and detailed information

![Level 3: a selected property, with its spaces on the left and details on the right](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4.jpg)

At LOD 3, visitors can examine a property's phases, buildings or spaces, with choices on the left and details on the right. The available actions depend on the property being shown. Future and Now both include wider district views and closer property views; Past uses LOD 3 to show the archive timeline, map and completion records together.

## Finding your way around the screen

| Area | What it contains |
|---|---|
| Left panel | Controls for choosing what to explore, followed by phases, buildings or units when a property is open |
| Map | The district, with the selected property kept in view |
| Right panel | Information about the selected property and the actions available for it |
| Map controls | Zoom, basemap and street-view controls |
| Header | Date, time and district conditions |

The map and property list are linked. Tapping a property on the map highlights it in the list; choosing its name from the list moves the map to that location. Both open the same property information.

When you open a property, the left panel changes to show its contents. Use the Back link above the list to return to the previous level or the district controls.

## Choosing what to explore

In the district overview, the left panel groups the controls under Exploration Mode, Timeline, Areas of Interest and Assets.

- **Exploration Mode** offers Free, Business and Research.
- **Timeline** switches between Future, Now and Past and provides the year controls.
- **Areas of Interest** takes you directly to a featured property.
- **Assets** groups map content by categories such as Office, Residential and Mobility.

Opening a property replaces these overview controls with its history, phases or units. If you need the timeline or asset filters again, use Back to return to the overview.

## Reading property details

The right panel usually starts with a property's status and name, followed by images, a description and the main figures. Camera or video tiles appear where provided, with actions such as Enquire, Building Info and View interior at the bottom. Building data and archive records use their own layouts because they present different kinds of information.

Property panels follow a similar order, making it easier to find the same details when comparing two buildings or phases.

## Understanding status labels

| Label | Meaning | How to follow up |
|---|---|---|
| Available | Space is offered for lease | Review the listed spaces, explore the property and enquire |
| Under offer | An offer is in progress, but the lease has not been signed | Ask the team about registering interest if it becomes available |
| Leased | No space is currently available | Explore the building and ask the team about future availability |
| Planned | A proposed property or phase, shown with a target year | Review the proposal and make an off-plan enquiry |
| Completed | A completed property shown in Past with its completion year | Read its record alongside the archive imagery |

Read these labels with the prototype's data status in mind. Availability still needs DSO's leasing feed, and target years for proposed phases remain indicative.

## Opening an enquiry

Enquire appears at the bottom of Future property panels and the available-space panel in Now. The exterior and office-interior tour examples also show this button.

The supplied leased-building and Past screens do not show Enquire. After exploring those screens, return to a proposed or available property to open its form, or discuss future availability in a leased building directly with the commercial team.
