Photos show a building at its best. Live cameras show it as it is. In Now, every built property has its CCTV cameras in the right panel, and the district has a video gallery. Use them to see the entrances, lobbies and common areas before you visit.

## Open a live camera

Select a CCTV tile in the right panel to open the camera full size. It shows the view right now, with the camera name, its zone and its live status.

![a CCTV camera open full size, with the live status and camera details](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-cctv-enlarge.jpg)

## Watch the district videos

Select a video tile to open the gallery full size. The thumbnail strip under the player moves between videos.

![the video gallery open full size, with the thumbnail strip](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-video-enlarge.jpg)
