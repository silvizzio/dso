The total area of a phase does not tell a tenant whether a building will suit their needs. They may want the whole team on one floor, need dedicated lab space or be looking for a shop at street level. The building details let them examine those questions before contacting the commercial team.

Start with a phase, open one of its parts and then choose a building. The map keeps the location visible as the panels show more detail.

## Opening a building

Open a phase to see its parts, or sub-phases, each containing a group of buildings. Select a part to highlight its boundary on the map. Building markers show the proposed use and height, and the same buildings appear in the left panel with a Back link above them.

![Part of a phase selected, with its boundary on the map and its buildings listed on the left](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-1.jpg)

Choose a building to see its floor count, typical floor area and mix of uses. The floor list runs from the highest level down to **G**, the ground floor, with the proposed use and area shown for each level.

![A selected building, showing its floors and their proposed uses](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-building-level.jpg)

Use Back to return through the lists if you want to compare another building or return to the whole complex.

## Comparing the space on each floor

The **typical floor plate** describes the area of a standard floor. It is useful for a company that wants to occupy a whole level, because two buildings with similar total areas can have very different floor sizes.

Read the floor areas together with their proposed uses. A lab tenant can identify the lab floors, while a retailer can look for street-level space. Service and plant areas also help explain the arrangement of the building and may raise questions about access or neighbouring uses.

These details help visitors narrow their options and explain their requirements more precisely. Sub-phase and building figures are still indicative; the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) lists the information DSO needs to confirm.

## Next
