Future opens on District IO as a whole. Here you can see what the complex contains, how its floor area splits between uses, and which homes it offers. This chapter explains how to read those figures, and what they tell you about the place District IO will be. Future is the default view, because District IO is the main offer.

<Callout type="warning" title="Projected figures">
All figures in Future are projected. The areas come from the DSO HQ Concept Design Stage Report. The report gives phases, not calendar dates. Delivery years on screen are indicative until DIEZ confirms the programme.
</Callout>

## Read off-plan figures as intent, not as a floor plan

District IO is at concept design stage, the first formal design stage. It fixes what goes where: the mix of uses, the size of each phase and the rough shape of the buildings. Later stages turn this into detailed layouts, and exact areas usually move a little along the way.

So read the figures in Future as the intent of the project. The mix and the phasing tell you what kind of place District IO will be. The exact area of a given office comes later, in the lease or sale terms.

## See how the floor area splits between uses

District IO is mixed use: different kinds of space share one site. For a tenant, this means food, shops and hotel rooms for visitors within walking distance. For a resident, it means the workplace is next door.

| Use | GFA (m²) | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial, office and techno lab | 409,000 | 69% |
| Residential | 64,000 | 11% |
| Retail | 50,000 | 8% |
| Hotel | 33,500 | 6% |
| Social and open space | 23,300 | 4% |
| Convention centre and museum | 15,500 | 3% |
| Total | 595,300 | 100% |

The split tells you what kind of place it will be. At 69%, office and techno lab space sets the character: this is a business district first. Techno lab space is built for light research and prototyping as well as office work. The residential share, 11%, is enough to give the complex evening and weekend life, but it does not make District IO a residential neighbourhood. Retail, the hotel and the convention centre serve the people who work and visit there.

## Check the homes on offer

All 704 homes are in Phase 1A, in four residential towers.

| Type | Homes |
|---|---|
| Studio | 171 |
| One bedroom | 316 |
| Two bedroom | 184 |
| Three bedroom | 33 |
| Total | 704 |

Studios and one bedroom homes make up 69% of the total. This mix suits people who work in the district, such as single professionals and couples. Families have 217 two and three bedroom homes.
