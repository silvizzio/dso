Past lets you move the timeline back and see the district as it was. The map switches to archived satellite images for the year you choose, and the panels show only what existed then. Step from 2010 to 2026 and watch roads, plots and buildings appear.

![the district in 2020: archived satellite imagery and the three buildings completed by that year](/dso/api/img/05-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

## Choose a year

Use the year steps in the left panel, or the arrows beside the timeline. The steps are 2010, 2015, 2020, 2025 and 2026.

The archived images are photographs of the site at that time, not renderings. What you see is what was there.

## See what changes on screen

| Region | What changes |
|---|---|
| Map | Archived satellite imagery for the selected year |
| Left panel | Only the areas that existed in that year, each with its completion year |
| Right panel | The Archive view: description, buildings over time, development records |

## Know what Past does not show

Past has no CCTV, no building data and no leasing status. None of these exist for an earlier year. District IO does not show in Past, because no part of it existed before today.
