Now shows the district as it is today. It shows only buildings that exist or are under construction. District IO is not built, so it is shown under Future.

## District overview

![Now overview: the areas of interest with their status, the district figures and the buildings over time](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-overview.jpg)

The right panel shows the district figures, a chart of buildings over time, and the video gallery.

## Leased property

When an area is fully leased, the right panel shows what the property is and its live CCTV.

![DSO HQ, leased: the history on the left, the leased spaces, and the CCTV on the right](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-leased.jpg)

| Region | Content |
|---|---|
| Left panel | The history of the building (construction, opening, today) and the spaces inside it with their status. |
| Right panel | Image gallery, description, completion year, levels, use, and the CCTV cameras. |

## Available property

When an area has space to lease, the left panel lists the spaces with their size and status.

![TechnoHub 4, available: suites and labs with size and status, and the key figures on the right](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-available-technohub-4.jpg)

| Region | Content |
|---|---|
| Left panel | The history of the building and each suite or lab with its area and status (Available or Under offer). |
| Right panel | Suites available, area range, rent from, occupancy, parking, completion year, CCTV, and the actions Enquire, Building Info and View interior. |

## Building level

Building Info opens the live building data from the building management system.

![TechnoHub 4 building level: systems status, comfort, and occupancy, temperature and energy load by floor](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-available-technohub-4-building-level.jpg)

| Item | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Systems online | The percentage of building systems that report data. |
| Zone comfort | The percentage of zones within the comfort range. |
| Retail, set point, load now | The retail space let, the temperature set point and the current energy load. |
| Floor by floor | Occupancy, temperature and energy load for each floor. |
| Energy | Energy use today against the typical day. |

## CCTV

Select a CCTV tile to open the camera full size.

![a CCTV camera opened full size, with the live status and the camera details](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-cctv-enlarge.jpg)

## Videos

Select a video tile to open the video gallery. The thumbnail strip moves between videos.

![the video gallery opened full size, with the thumbnail strip below](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-video-enlarge.jpg)

## Street view

Drag the pin from the map controls and drop it on a lit street. The lit streets are the routes where street view is available.

![dragging the pin: the walkable streets light up on the map](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-drag-and-place-pin.jpg)

When you drop the pin, the street view opens. Drag to look around. Select the arrow to walk towards the next building. The mini map shows where you are.

![the street view after the pin is placed, walking towards TechnoHub 4](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-placed-pin-exterior-virtual-tour.jpg)

## Interior tour

View interior opens a 360 degree tour inside the building. Drag to look around. Select the arrow to move to the next point.

![the interior tour inside a vacant office floor](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-virtual-tour-interior.jpg)
