A fully leased building has no space left to sell, so on a sales platform it could easily be treated as a dead end. The Digital Twin treats it as evidence instead.

A full building shows that DSO creates places tenants want to move into and stay in. It also shows the standard a future District IO tenant can expect. And a visitor who likes a full building is still a potential tenant for the next space that becomes available.

![a leased building opened: its history, the units inside it and its live cameras](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-leased.jpg)

## Full buildings prove demand and quality

Occupancy is one of the strongest signals in property. If tenants chose a building and then renewed their leases, it means both the location and the way the building is run are working. A visitor weighing up an off-plan decision can read that signal directly from the map.

The platform reinforces this with the building's history and live views. Visitors can see how long the building has been operating and what it contains, not just that it is full.

## Exploring a leased building in five steps

1. Select a leased building. Its status label shows that no space is currently available.
2. Read its history in the left panel, from construction through to today.
3. See what is inside: the units in the building, each with its use and status.
4. Watch the building live through its cameras in the right panel.
5. Enquire about future availability.

## Interest in a full building is still a lead

For visitors, a full building answers the question of whether there is real demand. It also shows the quality of a building DSO has been running for years, which matters to anyone thinking about District IO.

For the commercial team, an enquiry about a full building is still valuable. The team can offer the next space that becomes free there, or a similar space elsewhere in the district.

## Design decisions behind this view

- **History as well as status.** How long a building has been running, and what it contains, says much more about its quality than a single label.
- **No dead ends.** Enquire stays available on every full building, because a visitor's interest is real even when no unit is free.
