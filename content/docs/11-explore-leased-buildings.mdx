A fully leased building still has a place on the Digital Twin.
It is an example of DSO's completed work, and visitors may want to understand what it contains or enquire about future space.

For someone considering District IO, an existing building also provides context for the proposal.
They can examine a property already in use rather than relying only on plans for a future development.

![A leased building, showing its history, units and camera tiles](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-leased.jpg)

## What a leased property shows

The Leased label indicates that no space is currently available.
The building's history and unit information explain more about the property, including how long it has been operating and what it contains.

These details help visitors understand the existing district.
Occupancy is useful context, though it does not by itself explain tenants' experiences or why they chose the building.

## Exploring the building

Select a leased building on the map and check its status in the property panel.
The left panel shows its history from construction to the present, along with the units and their uses.

The right panel provides camera tiles for viewing the building where feeds are available.
Current camera content is placeholder material pending DSO's confirmation, as noted in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).
Building Info and View interior provide routes to further detail where those views are available.

## Exploring LIU 8

LIU 8 shows how the same layout works for light industrial space.
Its history and unit list sit on the left, while the right panel summarises the property and its next release.
The unit labels distinguish leased space from a future release under offer.

![LIU 8: leased industrial units, a future release and property details](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-liu-8-1.jpg)

The release date and availability are indicative until DSO confirms the leasing information.

## Following up on future space

The supplied leased-building screens do not show an Enquire button.
Visitors can use the building name and unit details when asking the commercial team about future space.
The team can discuss upcoming availability or suggest a similar property elsewhere in the district.
