A leased building has no space to sell, but it still matters to a buyer. It shows the quality DSO builds and runs, and it shows a building that is full and working.

![DSO HQ: the building history, the leased spaces, and the CCTV cameras](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-leased.jpg)

## See what the building is

DSO HQ is the example on screen:

- **Opened in 2005**, after construction started in 2003.
- **Holds** the offices of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority.
- **Includes** a customer centre and a 320 seat auditorium.

| Region | Content |

## Enquire for future availability

Space in a leased building becomes free when a lease ends. If you want to be in a particular building, select Enquire anyway. The DSO commercial team can tell you:

- when space is likely to come up in that building
- which similar spaces are available now
