Leased buildings remain on the Digital Twin because they are part of the district visitors are exploring. Their history, uses and viewing tools show examples of DSO's completed work, even where no space is currently offered for rent.

For someone considering District IO, these properties provide a way to examine buildings already in use. Someone interested in a particular leased building can also use its details when asking the team about future availability.

![A leased building, showing its history, units and camera tiles](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-leased.jpg)

## Exploring a leased property

Select a building on the map and check the **Leased** status in its property panel. The left panel shows its history from construction to the present, followed by units and their uses. These details explain what the building contains and how it has developed over time.

Camera tiles appear in the right panel where provided. The current footage is placeholder content pending the camera access described in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference). **Building Info** and **View interior** open further details where those actions are available.

A leased status describes availability; it does not, by itself, explain tenants' experiences or why they chose the property.

## The LIU 8 example

LIU 8 uses the same layout for light industrial space. Its history and unit list appear on the left, while the right panel summarises the property and its next release. Unit labels distinguish the leased space from a future release shown as under offer.

![LIU 8: leased industrial units, a future release and property details](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-liu-8-1.jpg)

The release date and availability remain indicative until DSO confirms the leasing information.

## Asking about future space

The supplied leased-building screens do not show an Enquire button. Note the building name and relevant unit details, then ask the commercial team about upcoming space or similar properties elsewhere in DSO.
