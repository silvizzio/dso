Past shows how the district grew. DSO HQ was completed first, in 2005. TechnoHub 4 followed in 2015 and LIU 8 in 2020. This is the delivery record behind District IO.

![the district in 2020: archived satellite imagery and the three buildings completed by that year](/dso/api/img/06-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

## Three buildings were delivered between 2005 and 2020

| Year | Property | What it added |
|---|---|---|
| 2005 | DSO HQ | The headquarters of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority |
| 2015 | TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs |
| 2020 | LIU 8 | Light industrial units |

## Past changes the map and both panels

| Region | What changes |
|---|---|
| Map | Archived satellite imagery for the selected year |
| Left panel | Only the areas that existed in that year, each with its completion year |
| Right panel | The Archive view: description, buildings over time, development records |

Use the year steps to move between 2010, 2015, 2020, 2025 and 2026.

## Past has no live data

Past has no CCTV, no building data and no leasing status. None of these exist for an earlier year. District IO does not show in Past.
