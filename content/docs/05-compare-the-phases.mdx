District IO opens in five phases. Select District IO to see each phase on the map, compare what it contains, and follow the delivery track. This helps you decide which phase fits you, and what the complex around it will look like when you move in.

![District IO: the delivery track, the five phases, and their markers on the map](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io.jpg)

## Understand what a phase is

A phase is a part of the complex that is designed, built and handed over as one package. Phasing lets a large project open in stages, so the first tenants move in before the last buildings are finished.

On screen, each phase appears in three places:

- **On the map**, as a marker with its expected completion.
- **In the left panel**, in the list of phases, in delivery order.
- **In the right panel**, with its figures, when you select it.

## Compare what each phase contains

| Phase | GFA (m²) | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | 194,707 | Office and techno lab, all the residential (704 homes), retail, open space |
| 1B | 123,807 | Office and techno lab, retail, open space |
| 1C | 128,987 | Office and techno lab, the largest Phase 1 retail, open space |
| 1D | 82,980 | Office and techno lab, retail, open space |
| 2 | 64,819 | Hotel (440 keys), convention centre, museum, retail, open space |

### How the phases differ

- **Phase 1A is the heart of the complex.**
  - It holds a third of all the floor area.
  - It has all the homes, the largest office block and the most open space.
  - It is designed to work as a place from the day it opens, not as the first corner of a building site.
- **Phases 1B to 1D add workplace.**
  - They are mainly office and techno lab space.
  - Phase 1C carries the most retail of the three.
- **Phase 2 adds destinations.**
  - It has no office at all.
  - It adds the hotel, the convention centre and the museum, which bring visitors to the district.

## Follow the delivery track

The delivery track at the top of the left panel shows four milestones:

1. **Concept**: the design stage the project is in now.
2. **Start**: construction begins.
3. **Complete**: construction finishes.
4. **Handover**: tenants and buyers get the keys, usually a few months after completion, once inspections and fit-out access are done.

On screen, the track reads Concept 2025, Start 2027, Complete 2030 and Handover 2031.

- These years are indicative.
- The concept report gives the phases but not their dates, so the programme is still for DIEZ to confirm.

## See the complex grow over time

1. Move the timeline to a year.
2. Read the map: phases built by that year show in full, and later phases show as planned.
3. Select a phase to see its figures for that stage.

This is the quickest way to see what will surround a unit when you move in, and what will still be under construction.
