After looking around District IO in Future, a sensible visitor tends to have two questions. What could I have today? And is what DSO builds actually any good?

Now answers both by showing the district exactly as it runs today. Visitors can see which spaces are free to lease, how the buildings are performing, and what the district looks like from the street and from the inside. Only things that already exist appear here. District IO stays in Future until construction starts, so what's real and what's planned never get mixed up.

## Showing instead of telling

A brochure can say the buildings are well run and in high demand. Now lets people check. Live building data shows how a building is actually operating. Live cameras show it as it is right now. Street view and the interior tours give a feel for what it would be like to arrive there in the morning and work there all day.

Visitors don't have to take anyone's word for any of this. They can look for themselves and decide.

## The existing buildings do the selling

Some visitors need space soon, and Now gives them what they came for: places they can move into today. But it also answers the bigger question sitting behind any off-plan purchase, which is whether this developer can be trusted.

That's why the buildings DSO already runs matter so much. How they perform, how full they are and what condition they're in make a stronger case for District IO than any render could.

## What you can do in Now

1. [Find Available Space](/dso/docs/09-find-available-space): see what's free today and put together a shortlist.
2. [Check How a Building Performs](/dso/docs/10-check-building-performance): look at a building's live operating data.
3. [Explore Leased Buildings](/dso/docs/11-explore-leased-buildings): look at full buildings as proof that people want to be here.
4. [Watch Cameras and Videos](/dso/docs/12-watch-cameras-and-videos): see the district live and watch videos about it.
5. [Walk the Street and the Interior](/dso/docs/13-walk-the-street-and-interior): visit the site at eye level without actually going there.

The first task is really for people who need space soon. The other four are worth everyone's time, including visitors who came mainly for District IO, because they show the standard DSO works to.

## Keeping Now honest

Nothing planned appears in Now, which keeps the evidence clean. Wherever live data exists, the platform uses it, because a live reading is much harder to argue with than a photo. And every property still ends with an Enquire button, full buildings included. Someone who likes a building with no free space is still a potential tenant.
