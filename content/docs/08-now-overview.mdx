After exploring District IO, visitors may want to know what DSO offers today and how its existing buildings are managed.
Now brings those questions into one view.
It shows existing properties, their leasing status and the tools for exploring them in more detail.

The district map may also show District IO as a proposed development, marked Planned.
Use Future to explore its plans, and distinguish that proposal from the existing properties offered in Now.

## Looking at buildings in use

Building data, camera feeds and virtual tours offer different ways to examine a property.
Data can help explain how a building is operating, while cameras show the areas they cover.
Street view and interior tours help visitors understand the approach to a building and the space inside it.

These views add context to photographs and descriptions.
The current screens include indicative data and placeholder cameras pending DSO's connections, as explained in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).

## Supporting immediate and future requirements

For visitors who need space sooner, Now provides a starting point for a leasing enquiry.
They can identify suitable properties and ask the commercial team about availability and terms.

Existing buildings are also relevant to visitors considering District IO.
They show examples of the properties DSO has developed and operates, helping visitors form questions about the proposed development.

## Exploring Now

1. [Find available space](/dso/docs/09-find-available-space) to compare properties and prepare a shortlist.
2. [Check how a building performs](/dso/docs/10-check-building-performance) to understand the building data view.
3. [Explore leased buildings](/dso/docs/11-explore-leased-buildings) to learn about existing properties and prepare questions about future availability.
4. [Watch cameras and videos](/dso/docs/12-watch-cameras-and-videos) to see the media available for a property.
5. [Walk the street and the interior](/dso/docs/13-walk-the-street-and-interior) to explore at eye level.

Visitors can use these tools in the order that suits their needs.
Someone looking for space soon may start with availability, while a District IO buyer may be more interested in the existing buildings.

## Keeping availability and data clear

Now separates existing properties from future plans.
Within it, visitors also need to distinguish connected live data from indicative figures and placeholder content.
Those labels explain what they can rely on and what still needs confirmation.

Available properties provide an Enquire action.
The leased-building views show the property history, cameras and viewing tools; visitors interested in future space can discuss the building with the commercial team.
