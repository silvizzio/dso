Now shows the district as it is today. It has two jobs: to show you where you can lease space right now, and to prove, with live data, cameras and tours, how the built stock performs. It shows only buildings that exist. District IO is not built, so it sits under Future until construction starts.

![Now overview: the areas of interest with their status, and the district summary on the right](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-overview.jpg)

## The three built properties

| Property | Type | Status | What you can do |
|---|---|---|---|
| TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs | Available | See each suite, view interior, enquire |
| DSO HQ | Headquarters | Leased | See the building and its spaces, enquire for future availability |
| LIU 8 | Light industrial | Leased | See the building, enquire for future availability |

Each property plays a different part:

- **TechnoHub 4 is the one you can act on today.**
- **DSO HQ and LIU 8 are proof**: buildings that are full and running.

## What the overview shows

- **Left panel**: the areas with their status, so you see at once where space is available.
- **Right panel**:
  - a chart of buildings over time
  - the district video gallery

## What you can do in Now

1. [Find Available Space](/dso/docs/09-find-available-space)
   - Compare the suites in an available building, and read the rent.
2. [Check How a Building Performs](/dso/docs/10-check-building-performance)
   - Open live data on comfort, occupancy and energy, floor by floor.
3. [Explore Leased Buildings](/dso/docs/11-explore-leased-buildings)
   - See what a full building is and how long it has run.
4. [Watch Cameras and Videos](/dso/docs/12-watch-cameras-and-videos)
   - See entrances and lobbies live, and watch the district videos.
5. [Walk the Street and the Interior](/dso/docs/13-walk-the-street-and-interior)
   - Walk the approach at eye level, then step inside an available space.

<Callout type="note" title="Indicative data">
Rent, availability, building data and CCTV feeds in Now are indicative until DSO connects the data feeds. See the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).
</Callout>
