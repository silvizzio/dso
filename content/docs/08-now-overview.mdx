After seeing District IO in Future, a careful visitor asks two questions.
What can I have today?
Can I trust the quality of what DSO builds?

Now answers both with the district as it runs today.
It shows what is free to lease, how the buildings perform, and what the district looks like at street level and inside.
It shows only what exists.
District IO stays in Future until construction starts, so the present is never mixed with the plan.

## Now proves the offer with what already runs

A brochure can state that buildings are well run and in demand.
Now shows it.
Live building data shows how a building operates.
Live cameras show it as it is.
Street view and interior tours show what arriving and working there would feel like.

The visitor does not have to take a claim on trust.
They inspect the evidence and reach their own view.

## The operating buildings become a sales asset

For buyers and tenants, Now answers the immediate need, which is space that can be taken today.
It also answers the deeper question behind any off-plan decision: is this developer worth trusting?

For DSO, the buildings it already runs make the case for District IO.
Their performance, their occupancy and their condition argue for the new complex more convincingly than a render.

## A visit through Now covers five tasks

1. [Find Available Space](/dso/docs/09-find-available-space): see where space is free today and build a shortlist.
2. [Check How a Building Performs](/dso/docs/10-check-building-performance): read the building's live operating data.
3. [Explore Leased Buildings](/dso/docs/11-explore-leased-buildings): see full buildings as proof of demand and quality.
4. [Watch Cameras and Videos](/dso/docs/12-watch-cameras-and-videos): see the district live, and watch its story.
5. [Walk the Street and the Interior](/dso/docs/13-walk-the-street-and-interior): visit the site at eye level without travelling to it.

The first task serves a visitor who needs space soon.
The other four serve every visitor, including those who came for District IO, by showing the standard DSO operates to.

## Three rules keep Now credible

- Only what exists appears in Now.
  Keeping plans out keeps the proof clean.
- Live data is used wherever it exists, because a live reading is harder to doubt than a photograph.
- Every property still ends in Enquire, including full buildings.
  A visitor who likes a full building is still a lead.
