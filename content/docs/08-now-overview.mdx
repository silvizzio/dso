After exploring District IO in Future, a thoughtful visitor usually has two questions. What can I have today? And can I trust the quality of what DSO builds?

Now answers both by showing the district as it runs today. Visitors can see which spaces are free to lease, how the buildings are performing, and what the district looks like both at street level and inside. Now shows only what already exists. District IO stays in Future until construction begins, so the present is never mixed up with the plan.

## Now backs up the offer with what already exists

A brochure can say that buildings are well managed and in high demand. Now lets visitors see it for themselves. Live building data shows how a building is operating, live cameras show it as it really is, and street view and interior tours give a sense of what it would be like to arrive and work there.

Visitors do not have to take anyone's word for it. They can look at the evidence and make up their own minds.

## Existing buildings become a sales tool

For buyers and tenants, Now meets their immediate need: space they can move into today. It also answers the bigger question behind any off-plan decision, which is whether this developer can be trusted.

For DSO, the buildings it already runs become the strongest argument for District IO. Their performance, occupancy and condition make the case for the new complex far more convincingly than any render.

## What visitors can do in Now

1. [Find Available Space](/dso/docs/09-find-available-space): see where space is free today and put together a shortlist.
2. [Check How a Building Performs](/dso/docs/10-check-building-performance): view a building's live operating data.
3. [Explore Leased Buildings](/dso/docs/11-explore-leased-buildings): look at fully leased buildings as proof of demand and quality.
4. [Watch Cameras and Videos](/dso/docs/12-watch-cameras-and-videos): see the district live, and watch videos about it.
5. [Walk the Street and the Interior](/dso/docs/13-walk-the-street-and-interior): visit the site at eye level without having to travel there.

The first task is for visitors who need space soon. The other four are useful to everyone, including those who came for District IO, because they show the standard DSO works to.

## How Now stays credible

- **Only what exists appears in Now.** Keeping plans out makes the evidence more convincing.
- **Live data is used wherever it is available,** because a live reading is much harder to doubt than a photograph.
- **Every property still leads to Enquire, even fully leased buildings.** A visitor who likes a full building is still a potential tenant.
