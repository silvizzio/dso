Now shows DSO's existing properties, including space offered for lease and buildings already occupied. Visitors can check a property's leasing status, explore its interior or look at the building information available in the prototype.

This is a useful starting point for someone who needs space sooner than District IO can offer. It also lets prospective District IO buyers examine examples of buildings DSO has already developed and operates.

District IO may still appear on the map with a **Planned** marker. That marker identifies the proposal among the existing buildings; switch to Future to explore its phases and programme.

## Looking beyond the property description

The tools in Now answer different questions about a building. Street view shows the approach and surroundings, while an interior tour gives a closer look at the space. Building Info is intended to present selected operating data, and cameras would show activity in the areas they cover once their feeds are connected.

The current screens contain indicative figures and placeholder camera content. They show how the platform is intended to work, but availability, live readings and feeds still need the data sources listed in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).

## Exploring existing properties

- [Find available space](/dso/docs/09-find-available-space) to compare unit sizes and prepare a shortlist for a leasing discussion.
- [Check how a building performs](/dso/docs/10-check-building-performance) to understand the readings and floor information shown in Building Info.
- [Explore leased buildings](/dso/docs/11-explore-leased-buildings) to learn about existing properties and identify questions about future space.
- [Watch cameras and videos](/dso/docs/12-watch-cameras-and-videos) to open the media provided for a property.
- [Walk the street and the interior](/dso/docs/13-walk-the-street-and-interior) to explore at eye level before arranging a visit.

Visitors can choose whichever task answers their immediate question. Available properties offer Enquire; the supplied leased-building screens show viewing tools instead. For future availability in a leased property, note its name and discuss it directly with the commercial team.
