A leased building has no space to sell, but it still matters to a buyer. It shows the quality DSO builds and runs, and it shows a building that is full and working. Select a leased building to see what it is, its history, the spaces inside it, and its live cameras.

![DSO HQ: the building history, the leased spaces, and the CCTV cameras](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-leased.jpg)

## See what the building is and how long it has run

DSO HQ opened in 2005 and holds the offices of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority, a customer centre and a 320 seat auditorium. The left panel shows its history from construction to today, and lists the spaces inside it with their status.

| Region | Content |
|---|---|
| Left panel | The building history (construction, opening, today) and each space with its status |
| Right panel | Description, completion year, levels, use, CCTV |

## Enquire for future availability

Space in a leased building becomes free when a lease ends. If you want to be in a particular building, select Enquire anyway. The DSO commercial team can tell you when space is likely to come up, and suggest similar space that is available now.
