The interface keeps the map in the centre. The panels sit on the left and right edges so that the map stays visible.

## LOD 1 and LOD 2

At LOD 1 and LOD 2 there are no side panels. The area markers sit on the map. The district figures sit in one band along the bottom of the screen.

![LOD 2: the district core, with District IO at the centre and the district figures along the bottom](/dso/api/img/02-lod2-dso-1.jpg)

## LOD 3

At LOD 3 the full interface opens.

![the full LOD 3 layout: left panel, map, map controls and right panel](/dso/api/img/03-now-lod3-overview.jpg)

| Region | What it does |
|---|---|
| Header | Shows the DSO logo, the compass heading, the environment readings (wind, sea level, air temperature, air quality), the date and time, and the user menu. |
| Left panel | Controls what the map shows: exploration mode, timeline, areas of interest, assets and innovations. When you open an area, it shows the list of items inside that area and a Back link. |
| Map | Shows the 3D district. Markers show the areas of interest. The selected building has a glowing outline. |
| Map controls | Timeline shortcut, basemap switch, drop pin, and zoom in and out. |
| Right panel | Shows the selected item: status label, title, image gallery, description, key figures, media, and the Enquire, Building Info and View interior actions. |

## Left panel controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Exploration mode | Free, Business or Research. The mode sets which stories and areas the kiosk puts first. |
| Timeline | Sets Now, Past or Future. Use the arrows or the year steps. |
| Areas of interest | Selects an area. The map flies to it and the right panel shows its details. |
| Assets | Shows asset types on the map: office, residential, commercial, education, retail, mobility, industrial and utilities. |
| Innovations | Shows innovation sites on the map, for example labs. |

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Available | Space you can lease now. |
| Under offer | Space with an offer in progress. |
| Leased | No space is available now. |
| Coming 2030 | Not built. Construction has not started. |
| Completed | Shown in Past. The year the building was completed. |
