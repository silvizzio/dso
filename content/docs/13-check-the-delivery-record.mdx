A buyer who commits off-plan takes a delivery risk: the risk that the building is late, changes, or is not built at all. The best evidence against that risk is what the developer has already built. Past lists that record, year by year.

## See what was delivered and when

| Year | Property | What it added |
|---|---|---|
| 2005 | DSO HQ | The headquarters of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority |
| 2015 | TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs |
| 2020 | LIU 8 | Light industrial units |

The three buildings are of three different kinds: a headquarters, a campus of offices and labs, and light industrial units. DSO has built each type and runs it today. District IO combines the first two at a larger scale, with homes and a hotel added.

## Read the development records

The development records in the right panel list what was completed in each year, and mark the most recent addition as new. The chart above them counts the featured buildings in each year. Read together with the archived imagery, they show both what DSO built and the district that grew around it.
