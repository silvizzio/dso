Start with District IO as a whole. Here you see what the complex contains, how its floor area splits between uses, and which homes it offers. Read together, these figures tell you what kind of place District IO will be.

## Read off-plan figures as intent

District IO is at concept design stage, the first formal design stage.

- **What concept design fixes:**
  - the mix of uses
  - the size of each phase
  - the rough shape of the buildings
- **What comes later:**
  - detailed layouts
  - exact areas, which usually move a little along the way

So read the figures in Future as the intent of the project, not as a floor plan. The exact area of a given office comes later, in the lease or sale terms.

## See how the floor area splits between uses

District IO is mixed use: different kinds of space share one site.

- **For a tenant**, food, shops and hotel rooms for visitors are within walking distance.
- **For a resident**, the workplace is next door.

| Use | GFA (m²) | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial, office and techno lab | 409,000 | 69% |
| Residential | 64,000 | 11% |
| Retail | 50,000 | 8% |
| Hotel | 33,500 | 6% |
| Social and open space | 23,300 | 4% |
| Convention centre and museum | 15,500 | 3% |
| Total | 595,300 | 100% |

### What the split tells you

- **Office and techno lab space sets the character.** At 69%, this is a business district first.
- **Homes give it life, not a new identity.** At 11%, the residential share keeps the complex active in the evening and at weekends, but it does not make District IO a residential neighbourhood.
- **The rest serves the people there.** Retail, the hotel and the convention centre are for those who work in and visit the district.

## Check the homes on offer

All 704 homes are in Phase 1A, in four residential towers.

| Type | Homes |
|---|---|
| Studio | 171 |
| One bedroom | 316 |
| Two bedroom | 184 |
| Three bedroom | 33 |
| Total | 704 |

- **Studios and one bedroom homes are 69% of the total.** This mix suits people who work in the district, such as single professionals and couples.
- **Families have 217 homes** with two or three bedrooms.
