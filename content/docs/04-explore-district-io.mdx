District IO is planned to include offices, techno labs, homes, shops, a hotel and public space. The phase list helps visitors find the parts relevant to them: a company looking for labs can start with the workplace phases, while someone interested in a home can look at the residential phases.

Each phase groups work intended to be designed, built and handed over together. Its uses and target year give visitors a starting point before they examine individual buildings or floors.

## Choosing a phase

Select District IO on the map or under Areas of Interest. The map moves to the complex and shows a marker for each phase, while the left panel lists the phases with their uses and target years. Markers combine **Planned** with a year, such as **Planned 2030**, to identify the proposed timing.

![District IO selected, with phase markers on the map and the phase list on the left](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io.jpg)

The current example divides the development as follows:

| Phase | Proposed uses |
|---|---|
| 1A | Offices and techno labs |
| 1B | Commercial space and a retail promenade |
| 1C | A hotel and convention centre |
| 1D | Residential and social space |
| 2 | A museum, central plaza and residential towers |

Several phases share a target year, so compare their uses as well as their dates. Tap a phase in the list or on the map to open it. The map then shows the parts within that phase, and the right panel displays its description and figures.

![A selected phase, with its parts on the map and its details in the right panel](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a.jpg)

## Understanding the phase figures

The right panel describes what the phase will contain and gives two measures of its size. **Gross floor area (GFA)** is the total floor space inside the buildings; **gross leasable area (GLA)** is the portion available to lease. A tenant assessing how much space could be rented should look at GLA alongside the proposed uses.

**Target Year** gives the proposed timing, while **Status**, such as Concept, identifies the design stage. These fields answer different questions: when the phase is expected and how far its design has progressed. Neither a target year nor a finished-looking model confirms that construction is complete.

## Exploring further

From a phase, you can open its buildings, compare the delivery years or select Enquire. The form is intended to include the selected phase when submission is connected, so the commercial team can see which part of District IO you want to discuss.

The map and list provide two ways to reach the same details. Use whichever is easier, and check the status of projected figures before relying on them for a property decision.
