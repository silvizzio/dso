District IO is too large to read as one block. It has 595,300 m² of floor area in five phases, and each phase has a different character. So the kiosk lets you explore it phase by phase: select District IO, pick a phase, and the map and the right panel show what that phase contains, how big it is, and where it stands.

This is how you find the part of the complex that fits you, before you look at a single building.

## Select District IO

1. In Future, select **District IO** in Areas of interest, or tap its marker on the map.
2. The map flies in, and a marker appears for each of the five phases.
3. The left panel shows the delivery track at the top, and the list of phases below it, in delivery order.

![District IO selected: the delivery track and the five phases in the left panel, and a marker for each phase on the map](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io.jpg)

Each phase in the list shows its name, its expected completion and a one-line summary. Read the list as a sequence, not a menu: Phase 1 (1A to 1D) builds a place to work and live, and Phase 2 then turns it into a destination. Where you enter that sequence decides what surrounds you when you move in.

## Pick a phase

Select a phase in the list, or tap its marker on the map.

![Phase 1A selected: its sub-phases on the map, and its components, floor areas, delivery and status in the right panel](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a.jpg)

Two things change:

- **The map** moves to the phase and shows its sub-phases, each with its own marker.
- **The right panel** shows the phase, top to bottom:
  1. the status label and the phase name
  2. an image gallery
  3. the components of the phase, by floor area
  4. its gross floor area (GFA) and gross leasable area (GLA)
  5. its delivery year and design status
  6. videos, and the Building Info and Enquire actions

### Read a phase: Phase 1A as an example

| Item | Phase 1A | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Components | Office and techno lab 114,581 m², residential 64,000 m², open space 9,570 m², retail 6,556 m² | What the phase is for, and in what proportion |
| GFA | 194,707 m² | The total floor area of the phase |
| GLA | 138,852 m² | The part a tenant can lease, here 71% of the floor area |
| Delivery | 2030 (indicative) | When the phase is expected to complete |
| Status | Concept | How far the design has progressed |

Read the components first. They tell you whether a phase is a workplace, a place to live, or a destination. Then read GLA rather than GFA if you plan to lease, because GLA is the space you pay for.

## What the phasing means for your choice

The phase figures only tell you what each phase contains. Put side by side, they also tell you what you trade when you choose one phase over another.

### An early phase gives you choice, a later phase gives you a finished setting

Phase 1A comes first. Entering there gives you the widest choice of space and the earliest move-in. The cost is time: the phases around you stay under construction for years, and the destinations, the hotel, the convention centre and the museum, arrive last, in Phase 2.

A later phase reverses the trade. You have less choice and a later date, but you move into a complex that is largely complete, with its neighbours built and running.

### Office space arrives in steady blocks, so growth can be planned

Four phases each add a similar block of office and techno lab space, between about 72,000 and 115,000 m². Supply does not arrive once and stop. A company that expects to grow can take space in an early phase and plan its expansion into a later phase next door, rather than moving out of the district.

### Homes open before most of the shops

Phase 1A has all 704 homes and 114,581 m² of offices, but only 6,556 m² of retail. Three quarters of all the retail in District IO (74%) comes later, in 1C, 1D and Phase 2.

- **For a home buyer**, early residents will do much of their daily shopping in the wider DSO district until the later phases open. The homes lean small, with 69% studios and one bedroom homes, which suits professionals who work nearby more than families.
- **For a retailer**, the few early units in 1A face the opposite position: a captive market of residents and office workers, with little competition on site. Later units compete with more retail but gain visitors from the destinations.

### The complex changes character as it grows

Phase 1 is a place to work and live. Phase 2 adds no office at all, only places to visit. By the time the hotel and the convention centre open, they open into a complex already full of tenants and residents, so they start with established demand rather than an empty site. For hospitality and events operators, that is the case for Phase 2 despite its later date.

<Callout type="note" title="Read the figures as intent">
District IO is at concept design stage. The components and areas show what each phase is for. Exact areas can move a little as the design develops, and the delivery years are indicative until DIEZ confirms the programme.
</Callout>
