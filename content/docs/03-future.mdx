District IO is Phase 3 of the DSO masterplan. It is at concept design stage, so a buyer or tenant commits before the buildings exist. That buyer needs three answers: what they get, where it sits in the complex, and when it is ready.

Future gives all three. District IO adds 595,300 m² of mixed-use floor area in five phases. Phase 1A comes first and holds all 704 homes. Office and techno lab space is 69% of the total. You can drill down from the complex to one building and its floors, then enquire. Future is the default view, because District IO is the main offer.

<Callout type="warning" title="Projected figures">
All figures in Future are projected. The areas come from the DSO HQ Concept Design Stage Report. The report gives phases, not calendar dates. Delivery years on screen are indicative until DIEZ confirms the programme.
</Callout>

## Buying off-plan means reading figures, not rooms

Concept design is the first formal design stage. It fixes what goes where: the mix of uses, the size of each phase and the rough shape of the buildings. Later stages turn this into detailed layouts, and exact areas usually move a little along the way.

So read the figures in this chapter as the intent of the project, not as a floor plan. The mix and the phasing tell you what kind of place District IO will be. The exact area of a given office comes later, in the lease or sale terms.

## District IO is a mixed-use complex

Mixed use means different kinds of space share one site: offices, homes, shops, a hotel and public space. For a tenant, it means food, shops and hotel rooms for visitors within walking distance. For a resident, it means the workplace is next door.

| Use | GFA (m²) | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial, office and techno lab | 409,000 | 69% |
| Residential | 64,000 | 11% |
| Retail | 50,000 | 8% |
| Hotel | 33,500 | 6% |
| Social and open space | 23,300 | 4% |
| Convention centre and museum | 15,500 | 3% |
| Total | 595,300 | 100% |

The split tells you what kind of place it will be. At 69%, office and techno lab space sets the character: this is a business district first. The residential share, 11%, is enough to give the complex evening and weekend life, but it does not make District IO a residential neighbourhood. Retail, the hotel and the convention centre serve the people who work and visit there.

## Five phases deliver it

A phase is a part of the complex that is designed, built and handed over as one package. Phasing lets a large project open in stages, so the first tenants move in before the last buildings are finished.

| Phase | GFA (m²) | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | 194,707 | Office and techno lab, all the residential (704 homes), retail, open space |
| 1B | 123,807 | Office and techno lab, retail, open space |
| 1C | 128,987 | Office and techno lab, the largest Phase 1 retail, open space |
| 1D | 82,980 | Office and techno lab, retail, open space |
| 2 | 64,819 | Hotel (440 keys), convention centre, museum, retail, open space |

Phase 1A is the largest phase and the most complete on its own. It holds a third of all the floor area, all the homes, the largest office block and the most open space. It is designed to work as a place from the day it opens, not as the first corner of a building site.

Phases 1B to 1D add mainly office and techno lab space, with 1C carrying the most retail of the three. Phase 2 is different in kind. It has no office at all and adds the hotel, the convention centre and the museum: the destinations that bring visitors to the district.

## Homes come in four types

All 704 homes are in Phase 1A, in four residential towers.

| Type | Homes |
|---|---|
| Studio | 171 |
| One bedroom | 316 |
| Two bedroom | 184 |
| Three bedroom | 33 |
| Total | 704 |

Studios and one bedroom homes make up 69% of the total. This mix suits people who work in the district, such as single professionals and couples. Families have 217 two and three bedroom homes.

## From concept to handover

The delivery track on screen shows four milestones. **Concept** is the design stage the project is in now. **Start** is when construction begins. **Complete** is when construction finishes. **Handover** is when tenants and buyers get the keys, usually a few months after completion, once inspections and fit-out access are done.

On screen, the track reads Concept 2025, Start 2027, Complete 2030 and Handover 2031. These years are indicative. The concept report gives the phases but not their dates, so the programme is still for DIEZ to confirm.

## You drill down in four steps, from district to building

The kiosk lets you go from the whole complex to a single floor in four taps. Each step keeps the one before it in view, so you always know where you are.

<StepList>
<Step number={1}>Select **District IO** to see the five phases and the delivery track.</Step>
<Step number={2}>Select a **phase** to see its sub-phases.</Step>
<Step number={3}>Select a **sub-phase** to see its buildings.</Step>
<Step number={4}>Select a **building** to see its levels, floor area and use mix.</Step>
</StepList>

### Step 1: District IO shows the phases and the delivery track

![District IO: the delivery track, the five phases, and their markers on the map](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io.jpg)

Each phase has a marker on the map with its expected completion. The left panel lists the phases in delivery order, and the right panel shows the figures for the selected phase.

Move the timeline to a year to see which phases exist in that year. Phases that are not built yet in that year show as planned. This is the quickest way to see how the complex will grow around a unit you are interested in.

![District IO at 2030: phases delivered by 2030, and later phases shown as planned](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-2030-some-phases-not-built-yet.jpg)

### Step 2: a phase shows its sub-phases

![Phase 1A: sub-phases 1A-1, 1A-2 and 1A-3 on the map, and the phase figures on the right](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a.jpg)

Sub-phases group the buildings of a phase by what they contain. In Phase 1A, two sub-phases hold the office clusters and the third holds the homes and open space.

| Sub-phase | What it contains |
|---|---|
| 1A-1 | Office and techno lab cluster |
| 1A-2 | Second office cluster |
| 1A-3 | Residential blocks and open space |

### Step 3: a sub-phase shows its buildings

![Phase 1A-1: Techno Lab Block and Office Tower A on the map](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-1.jpg)

The selected sub-phase has a glowing outline on the map, and each building in it has its own marker with its use and height.

### Step 4: a building shows its design data

![Techno Lab Block: levels, building GFA, typical floor plate, use mix, and floor by floor areas](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-building-level.jpg)

This is the level a tenant uses to judge fit. The typical floor plate is the area of one standard floor, and it tells you how much space you get if you take a whole floor. The floor by floor list shows what each floor is for, so you can see where the lab floors, the retail and the plant sit.

| Item | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Levels | Number of floors, for example G+5 |
| Building GFA | Total floor area of the building |
| Typical plate | Floor area of a typical floor |
| Use mix | Share of floor area by use |
| Floor by floor | Use and area of each floor |

<Callout type="note" title="Indicative data">
Sub-phase and building data are indicative. The concept report gives areas by phase, not by building.
</Callout>
