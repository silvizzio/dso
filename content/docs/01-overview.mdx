The DSO Digital Twin is a touch kiosk for Dubai Silicon Oasis. It runs on a 55 inch 4K touch screen. It shows the district as a 3D map. A visitor uses it to explore the district, find a property, look inside the property, and send an enquiry.

The kiosk is a sales tool for the DSO commercial team. It supports the Director General demo and the November opening.

![the district at LOD 1, with the four areas of interest on the map and the district figures along the bottom](/dso/api/img/02-lod1.jpg)

## What a visitor does

<StepList>
<Step number={1}>**Explore.** Move around the district map and change the timeline.</Step>
<Step number={2}>**Discover.** Select an area of interest to see its status, figures and media.</Step>
<Step number={3}>**Immerse.** Open the building level, the street view or the interior tour.</Step>
<Step number={4}>**Enquire.** Send contact details to the DSO commercial team from any property.</Step>
</StepList>

## Areas of interest

The kiosk has four areas of interest. Each area has a status label.

| Area | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| District IO | DSO HQ Complex, masterplan Phase 3 | Coming 2030 |
| DSO HQ | Headquarters of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority | Leased |
| TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices | Available |
| LIU 8 | Light industrial units | Leased |

## Timeline

The timeline sets which version of the district the kiosk shows. Each state shows different data.

| State | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Now | The district today. Only built stock, with leasing status, live building data and CCTV. |
| Past | Archived satellite imagery for a past year, and the buildings that existed in that year. |
| Future | The planned district. District IO and its phases, with projected figures. |

## Levels of detail

The map has three levels of detail (LOD). Each level shows more detail and more interface.

| Level | What the visitor sees |
|---|---|
| LOD 1 | The whole district from above, with the district figures along the bottom. |
| LOD 2 | A closer view of the district core around District IO. |
| LOD 3 | The full interface: left panel, map, right panel and map controls. |
