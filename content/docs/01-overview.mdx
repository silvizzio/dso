Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) has supported technology companies as a free zone since 2005. Its property portfolio includes existing buildings with space available to rent and others that are fully leased. District IO, by contrast, is still a proposal at the concept design stage, so prospective buyers and tenants cannot yet visit the development or experience it for themselves.

The DSO Digital Twin is designed to bridge that gap. It shows District IO on a 3D map alongside DSO's existing buildings, helping visitors understand how the proposal fits into the wider district. Using a touch screen, they can explore the development phases, view individual buildings and open an enquiry form in one place.

This guide walks through that experience using the current prototype screens. Some features, including leasing information, camera feeds, building data and enquiry routing, still depend on the connections or confirmation described in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).

## Understanding a property before it is built

For someone buying or leasing off-plan, the building itself is only part of the decision. They also need to know how to reach it, what is nearby and how much of the surrounding development will be ready when they move in. A brochure or a render of the finished complex may not answer those questions.

On the Digital Twin, visitors can follow District IO from the whole complex down to a phase, a building and its individual floors. They can compare the proposed uses and areas, then move through the target years to see how the development is expected to take shape. Existing buildings and archive imagery provide examples of DSO's completed work alongside those plans.

## Exploring Future, Now and Past

The platform opens with a wide view of the district. Future is the default timeline setting because District IO is the main development being presented, but visitors can switch to Now or Past at any point.

| View | What it shows | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Future | District IO's proposed phases, buildings, floors and target years | To assess off-plan space and prepare questions about the proposal |
| Now | Existing properties, leasing information, building data and tours | To look for space to rent or explore buildings already in use |
| Past | Archive imagery and records of completed projects | To see how DSO has developed over time |

These views support different conversations. A company looking to move soon may start with available space in Now. Someone considering District IO may explore the proposal first, then look at existing properties and earlier projects to learn more about DSO's experience.

## From exploring a property to making an enquiry

The Enquire button opens a form from a proposed property in Future or available space in Now. The intended process sends the selected property along with the visitor's contact details, giving the commercial team a specific place to start the conversation. The submission destination still needs to be connected; the prototype does not yet establish that an enquiry reaches the team.

At an exhibition stand or sales centre, staff can use the same map, property details and tours to answer questions as visitors explore. Building data and cameras are intended to add information about day-to-day operations once DSO supplies the required connections. See the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) for the status of each source.

## Terms used in this guide

- **Digital twin:** a 3D model of a real place linked to information about it. Here, the model represents Dubai Silicon Oasis and the properties featured in the platform.
- **Timeline state:** one of the three time settings, Future, Now or Past.
- **Level of detail (LOD):** one of the map's three zoom levels. More property information appears as visitors move closer.
- **Area of interest (AOI):** one of the four featured properties: District IO, DSO HQ, TechnoHub 4 and LIU 8.
- **Phase:** a section of District IO planned to be designed, built and handed over as one package.
