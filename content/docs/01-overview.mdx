The DSO Digital Twin shows what you can lease or buy at Dubai Silicon Oasis, when it will be ready, and what it contains. You can send an enquiry from the same screen. It runs on a 55 inch touch kiosk and shows the district as a 3D map.

The main offer is **District IO**, a 595,300 m² mixed-use complex in five phases. Three built properties, DSO HQ, TechnoHub 4 and LIU 8, show the delivery record behind it.

![the district at LOD 1, with the four areas of interest and the district figures along the bottom](/dso/api/img/02-lod1.jpg)

## This guide serves three kinds of reader

| Reader | What you get | Start with |
|---|---|---|
| Buyers and tenants | What is available now, what is coming and when, and how to enquire | Future, Now, Enquiry |
| DSO commercial team | How to use the screen and run a demo | Interface Guide, Demo and Data |
| DSO data and operations teams | Where each figure comes from, and what is still pending | Demo and Data |

## The kiosk answers four questions

Each question has one place in the kiosk. The questions do not overlap.

| Question | Where the kiosk answers it |
|---|---|
| What is here today, and what can I lease now? | Now |
| What is coming, and when? | Future |
| How did the district grow? | Past |
| How do I act on what I see? | Enquiry |

## District IO is the main offer

District IO is the DSO HQ Complex, Phase 3 of the DSO masterplan. CPG Middle East designed it. It is at concept design stage.

| Figure | Value |
|---|---|
| Gross floor area (GFA) | 595,300 m² |
| Gross leasable area (GLA) | 429,000 m² |
| Plot area | 164,420 m², five amalgamated plots |
| Homes | 704 |
| Hotel keys | 440 |
| Maximum height | G+40 |
| Stage | Concept design |

Office and techno lab space is 69% of the floor area. Homes, retail, a hotel, a convention centre, a museum and open space make up the rest. The Future chapter gives the full breakdown.

## Three built properties show the delivery record

| Property | What it is | Completed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| DSO HQ | Headquarters of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority | 2005 | Leased |
| TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs | 2015 | Available |
| LIU 8 | Light industrial units | 2020 | Leased |

## A visit follows four steps

<StepList>
<Step number={1}>**Explore.** Move around the district map and set the timeline to Now, Past or Future.</Step>
<Step number={2}>**Discover.** Select an area of interest to see its status, figures and media.</Step>
<Step number={3}>**Immerse.** Open the building level, the street view or the interior tour.</Step>
<Step number={4}>**Enquire.** Send your details and the property you looked at to the DSO commercial team.</Step>
