Dubai Silicon Oasis has hosted technology companies since 2005. DSO HQ, TechnoHub 4 and LIU 8 are built, and two of the three are fully leased. The next step is **District IO**, a 595,300 m² mixed-use complex in five phases. It is not built yet.

That is the problem for anyone who wants to lease or buy there. You cannot walk through a building that does not exist, and you must trust that it will be delivered.

So how do you judge District IO before it is built, and decide with confidence?

The DSO Digital Twin answers that on one screen. It shows District IO in 3D with its phases and figures. It shows what you can lease today and how the built stock performs. It shows what DSO has delivered since 2005. And it lets you send an enquiry from any property. It runs on a 55 inch touch kiosk.

![the district at LOD 1, with the four areas of interest and the district figures along the bottom](/dso/api/img/02-lod1.jpg)

## The kiosk tells the story in five beats

A visit moves from the offer to the proof to the action. Each beat has one place in the kiosk.

| Beat | What the visitor learns | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | The scale of the district: homes, units, transport and parking | LOD 1 |
| 2. Offer | What District IO contains, phase by phase, down to one building | Future |
| 3. Proof today | What exists now, how it performs, and what you can lease | Now |
| 4. Proof of record | What DSO has delivered since 2005 | Past |
| 5. Action | Send your details and the property you looked at | Enquire, in every state |

This is why the timeline runs Future, Now, Past: the offer comes first, then the proof.

## The timeline answers three questions

Each question has one timeline state. Future is the default state, because District IO is the main offer.

| Question | Timeline state |
|---|---|
| What is coming, and when? | Future |
| What is here today, and what can I lease now? | Now |
| How did the district grow? | Past |

Every property, phase and building has an Enquire button, in every state.

## District IO is the main offer

District IO is the DSO HQ Complex, Phase 3 of the DSO masterplan. CPG Middle East designed it. It is at concept design stage.

| Figure | Value |
|---|---|
| Gross floor area (GFA) | 595,300 m² |
| Gross leasable area (GLA) | 429,000 m² |
| Plot area | 164,420 m², five amalgamated plots |
| Homes | 704 |
| Hotel keys | 440 |
| Maximum height | G+40 |
| Stage | Concept design |

Office and techno lab space is 69% of the floor area. Homes, retail, a hotel, a convention centre, a museum and open space make up the rest. The Future chapter gives the full breakdown.
