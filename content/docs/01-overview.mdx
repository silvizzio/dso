Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) has been a free zone for technology companies since 2005.
The featured existing properties include leased buildings and space available to rent, while District IO is presented as a proposed development at the concept design stage.
Prospective buyers and tenants need to assess a place they cannot yet visit.

The DSO Digital Twin helps them do that.
It places District IO on a 3D map alongside the district's existing buildings, so visitors can explore the proposal in context.
The intended workflow lets them compare phases, look at individual buildings and send an enquiry from the same touch screen.
This guide describes that workflow using the current prototype screens. Leasing data, camera feeds, building readings and enquiry routing still need the connections or confirmation listed in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).

## What buyers need beyond a brochure

Buying or leasing off-plan means making a decision before a building is complete.
Drawings, projected figures and a sales conversation help explain the proposal, but they leave some practical questions unanswered.

Where will the building sit in relation to its neighbours?
How will people reach it, and how much of the surrounding development will be ready when they move in?
A render of the finished building may not show those details.

Buyers also need a way to assess the developer's experience.
Seeing what DSO has already completed gives them useful context for the plans they are considering.
Finally, visitors need a straightforward way to get in touch while a property is still fresh in their minds.

## How the platform helps

| Question | What the platform shows | How it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Where is the property? | Buildings on a shared map of the district | Visitors can see the location, surrounding buildings and access routes |
| What has DSO delivered? | Future plans, the present-day district and earlier years | Visitors can compare the proposal with existing buildings and completed projects |
| How do I follow up? | An Enquire button on the Future property views and available space in Now | Visitors can send an enquiry with their selection attached |

These features support different parts of the same decision: understanding the property, assessing it and asking for more information.

## What buyers and the DSO team gain

Buyers can explore District IO at their own pace, from the whole complex to individual floors.
They can then look at existing buildings, their operating data and virtual tours, or use the archive to review DSO's completed projects.
The enquiry form lets them contact the team when they are ready.

For the commercial team, each enquiry includes the property or part of the development that caught the visitor's attention.
This gives the first conversation a useful starting point, whether the interest is in a whole complex, one phase or a particular building.
The platform also presents the same information regardless of who is staffing the exhibition stand or sales centre.

For DSO as an operator, existing buildings provide examples of its work.
Building data, cameras and tours are intended to show how those properties operate, alongside the proposal for District IO.
The [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) explains which data is confirmed and which connections are still needed.

## Why the guide follows Future, Now and Past

A visit begins with a wide view of the district before introducing the detailed interface.
Future is the default timeline state because District IO is the main offer.
Visitors can explore the proposed complex, its phases and individual buildings before looking at the evidence behind it.

Now shows the district in use, including space to lease and information about existing buildings.
Past shows how the district developed over time.
Visitors can switch between these views, then use Enquire from a proposed or available property when they want to discuss it.

This order follows a useful sales conversation: explain the offer, answer questions about it and make it easy to follow up.
Visitors can also move directly to the section that matters to them.

## Terms used in this guide

A **digital twin** is a 3D model of a real place connected to information about it.
Here, it means the model of Dubai Silicon Oasis and the data the platform presents with it.

A **timeline state** is one of the three views of the district: Future, Now or Past.

A **level of detail (LOD)** is one of the three map zoom levels.
More information appears as the visitor moves closer.

An **area of interest (AOI)** is one of the four featured properties: District IO, DSO HQ, TechnoHub 4 and LIU 8.

A **phase** is a section of District IO designed, built and handed over as one package.
