Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) is a free zone for technology companies in Dubai. It has hosted tenants since 2005. Three of its buildings feature in the kiosk: DSO HQ, TechnoHub 4 and LIU 8. Two of the three are fully leased.

The next step is **District IO**, a 595,300 m² mixed-use complex in five phases. It is at concept design stage: the mix of uses, the phases and the floor areas are set, but the buildings do not exist yet. Anyone who leases or buys there now does so off-plan. They commit on the strength of drawings, figures and the developer's record, not on a building they can visit.

That is the gap the DSO Digital Twin closes. How do you judge a place you cannot walk through, and how do you trust that it will be built? The kiosk answers with three kinds of evidence on one screen: what District IO will contain, what DSO runs today, and what DSO has delivered before. When you have seen enough, you send an enquiry from the property you are looking at.

## The kiosk tells the story in five beats

A good sales conversation moves from the offer to the proof to the ask. The kiosk follows the same order, so a visitor who only follows the default screens still hears the whole story.

| Beat | What the visitor learns | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | The scale of the district: homes, units, transport and parking | LOD 1 |
| 2. Offer | What District IO contains, phase by phase, down to one building | Future |
| 3. Proof today | What exists now, how it performs, and what you can lease | Now |
| 4. Proof of record | What DSO has delivered since 2005 | Past |
| 5. Action | Send your details and the property you looked at | Enquire, in every state |

This order explains a choice that can look odd at first: the timeline runs Future, Now, Past, not Past, Now, Future. A visitor comes for what they can have, so the kiosk shows the offer first. The present and the past then work as proof.

## District IO is the main offer

District IO is Phase 3 of the DSO masterplan, also called the DSO HQ Complex. CPG Middle East designed it. It joins five plots into one site of 164,420 m².

| Figure | Value |
|---|---|
| Gross floor area (GFA) | 595,300 m² |
| Gross leasable area (GLA) | 429,000 m² |
| Plot area | 164,420 m², five amalgamated plots |
| Homes | 704 |
| Hotel keys | 440 |
| Maximum height | G+40 |
| Stage | Concept design |

Three things stand out in these figures.

**It is a workplace first.** Office and techno lab space is 69% of the floor area. Techno lab space is built for light research and prototyping as well as office work, which suits the technology companies DSO serves.

**It is also a place to live and stay.** The 704 homes and the 440 key hotel keep the complex active after office hours and at weekends, which a pure office park does not.

**It is dense.** The floor area is about 3.6 times the plot area, and the tallest building reaches G+40, a ground floor plus 40 floors.

The two area figures measure different things, and the difference matters when you compare offers. Gross floor area (GFA) is all the floor inside the buildings. Gross leasable area (GLA) is the part a tenant can rent. In District IO, 72% of the floor area is leasable. The rest is lobbies, lifts, stairs, corridors and plant rooms. When you compare two properties, compare GLA with GLA.

## Three built properties show the delivery record

These are the buildings a visitor can walk into today. They matter to a District IO buyer for two reasons: they show the quality DSO builds and runs, and they show that DSO completes what it starts.

| Property | What it is | Completed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| DSO HQ | Headquarters of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority | 2005 | Leased |
| TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs | 2015 | Available |
| LIU 8 | Light industrial units | 2020 | Leased |

## This guide serves two kinds of reader

| Reader | What you get | Start with |
|---|---|---|
| Buyers and tenants | What is coming and when, what is available now, and how to enquire | Future, Now |
| DSO teams | How to use the screen, and where each figure comes from | Interface Guide, Reference |

If you are here to lease or buy, read Future, then Now, then Past, in the same order as the kiosk. If you run or support the kiosk, the Interface Guide explains every control, and the Reference lists where each figure comes from and which figures are still indicative.
