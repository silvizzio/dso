Enquire lets visitors ask about a property while they are still looking at it. The form opens over the map from a complex, phase, part of a phase or individual building, and is intended to include that property with the visitor's contact details.

An off-plan enquiry starts a discussion about a property that has not yet been built. It does not reserve the space, require a deposit or create a contract. The commercial team would use the enquiry to discuss availability, timing and terms.

![The enquiry form over the map, showing property fields, contact details and an optional message](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-form.jpg)

## Filling in the form

1. Select **Enquire** from the property panel.
2. Check the property summary at the top of the form against the property you want to discuss.
3. Enter your name, company, email address and phone number.
4. Add an optional message, such as the amount of space you need, its intended use or your preferred move-in date.
5. Use the form's **Enquire** button to submit once the submission destination has been connected.

The map remains visible behind the form, so visitors can refer to the property as they enter their details. The four contact fields give the team a way to respond, while the message allows visitors to explain a requirement that the property selection alone would not convey.

## What still needs to be connected

The current form is a prototype. Its property details include placeholders: the example is headed DSO HQ even though a District IO phase is open behind it. The name and summary need to match the selected property before release.

DSO also needs to confirm where submissions should go, whether to a CRM or an email destination. Until that connection is in place, the screen demonstrates the intended enquiry process rather than a confirmed route to the commercial team. A QR code for continuing on a phone is planned but is not shown in the current form. Both the submission destination and QR link are listed in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).

Once connected, the process is intended to give the team the visitor's contact details, message and selected property together. That allows the first conversation to start with the building or phase the visitor has already explored.
