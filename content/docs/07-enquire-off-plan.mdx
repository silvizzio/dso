A visitor's interest is at its peak the moment they find something that suits them, and it drops quickly once they walk away from the screen. Traditional off-plan sales ask people to follow up later through a website, a phone call or an email, and many of them simply never get round to it.

The platform captures interest at the moment it happens. The Enquire action is available on every phase, part and building, and it opens a short form that already includes whatever the visitor was looking at.

![the enquiry form over the map, carrying the item the visitor selected](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-form.jpg)

## An enquiry expresses interest, not commitment

An **off-plan enquiry** registers interest in a property that has not been built yet. It is not a reservation, a deposit or a contract. It simply opens a conversation with the DSO commercial team about terms, timing and availability.

Keeping this step light is intentional. People often hesitate before committing to something, but they rarely hesitate before asking a question. A low-pressure action turns far more visitors into leads.

## The enquiry keeps the visitor's context

1. Tap Enquire on whatever is on screen, whether that is the whole complex, a phase, a part or a single building.
2. The form opens over the map, with the selected item and its key details shown at the top.
3. Enter your contact details: name, company, email and phone number.
4. If you like, add a short message about what you need, such as the floor area, the intended use or your preferred timing.
5. Send the enquiry. It goes to the DSO commercial team with the selected item attached.

Visitors who would rather continue later can scan the QR code on screen and pick up where they left off on their own phone.

## The form only asks for what the team needs

Every extra field on a form causes some people to give up. So the form asks for just the four details the commercial team needs in order to reply, plus an optional message.

The selected item takes care of the rest. The team never has to ask what the visitor was interested in, because the enquiry already tells them.

## Every enquiry arrives with context

For a buyer, taking action takes only a few seconds. There is no website to look up later, and nothing to explain twice.

For the commercial team, each enquiry comes with its context already attached. The team can see whether the visitor asked about the whole complex or a single floor in one building, so they can prepare a reply that addresses the real question rather than sending out a general brochure.

## Design decisions behind the enquiry

- **Enquire is always in the same place,** at the bottom of every right panel, so visitors never have to search for it.
- **The map stays visible behind the form,** so visitors never feel they have left what they were looking at.
- **The form is short,** because its job is to start a conversation, not to close a sale.
