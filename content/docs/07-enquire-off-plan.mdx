A visitor's interest is highest at the moment they find something that fits.
It falls quickly once they walk away from the screen.
Traditional off-plan sales ask the visitor to follow up later, through a website, a call or an email, and many never do.

The platform captures interest at the moment it forms.
The Enquire action sits on every phase, part and building.
It opens a short form that already carries what the visitor was looking at.

![the enquiry form over the map, carrying the item the visitor selected](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-form.jpg)

## An off-plan enquiry records interest, not commitment

An **off-plan enquiry** registers interest in a property that is not built yet.
It is not a reservation, a deposit or a contract.
It starts a conversation with the DSO commercial team about terms, timing and availability.

Keeping the step light is deliberate.
A visitor hesitates before committing, but rarely before asking a question.
A low-commitment action converts more visitors into leads.

## The enquiry carries the visitor's context

1. Tap Enquire on whatever is on screen: the whole complex, a phase, a part or a building.
2. The form opens over the map.
   It shows the selected item and its key details at the top.
3. Enter contact details: name, company, email and phone number.
4. Optionally, add a short message about what is needed, such as the floor area, the use or the timing.
5. Send.
   The enquiry goes to the DSO commercial team with the selected item attached.

A visitor who prefers to continue later can scan the QR code on screen and carry on from their own phone.

## The form asks only for what the team needs to reply

Every extra field on a form loses a share of visitors.
So the form asks for four details the commercial team needs in order to reply, plus one optional message.

The selected item does the rest.
The team does not need to ask what the visitor was interested in, because the enquiry already says so.

## Each enquiry arrives qualified

For a buyer, acting takes seconds.
There is no website to find later and nothing to explain twice.

For the commercial team, each enquiry arrives with its context attached.
The team knows whether the visitor asked about the whole complex or one floor of one building.
It can prepare a reply that answers the real question, rather than a general brochure.

## Three design decisions shape the enquiry

- Enquire is always in the same place, at the bottom of every right panel, so a visitor never has to search for it.
- The map stays visible behind the form, so the visitor never feels they have left what they were looking at.
- The form is short, because its purpose is to start a conversation, not to complete a sale.
