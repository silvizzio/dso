A visitor who finds a suitable property should be able to ask about it while the details are still in front of them.
Asking them to find a website or make a separate call later adds another step to the process.

Enquire opens a short form from the complex, phase, part or building the visitor is viewing.
The selection is included automatically, so they do not need to describe it again.

![The enquiry form over the map, with the selected item already included](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-form.jpg)

## What an off-plan enquiry means

An off-plan enquiry registers interest in a property that has not yet been built.
It opens a discussion about availability, timing and terms with the DSO commercial team.
Submitting the form does not reserve a property, require a deposit or create a contract.

That distinction should be clear before visitors send their details.
They are asking for information so they can decide what to do next.

## Completing the form

Tap Enquire while viewing the whole complex, a phase, part of a phase or a building.
The form opens over the map with the selected item's details at the top.

Enter your name, company, email address and phone number.
You can also add a message explaining what you need, such as the amount of space, its intended use or your preferred move-in date.
The message is optional.

Send the form to pass those details and the selection to the commercial team.
The intended QR option lets visitors continue from their phone if they prefer to finish later.
DSO still needs to confirm the enquiry destination and QR link, as listed in the [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference).

## Why the form asks for only a few details

The four contact fields give the team a way to follow up, while the optional message lets visitors explain a requirement in their own words.
The selected property supplies the context.
Keeping these details together reduces the amount a visitor has to enter and helps the team prepare a relevant reply.

## What happens after a visitor enquires

The intended process gives the commercial team both contact details and a specific area of interest.
They can see whether the visitor was exploring the complex, a phase or a particular building and respond to that selection.

Enquire stays in the same position at the bottom of the right panel.
The map remains visible behind the form, helping visitors keep track of the property while they complete it.
