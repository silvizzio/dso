When something in District IO fits, send an enquiry from the screen you are on. The form carries what you selected, so the DSO commercial team knows exactly what you looked at.

## Know what an off-plan enquiry is

- **It is** a way to register your interest before the building exists, and to start a conversation with the DSO commercial team about terms, timing and availability.
- **It is not** a commitment, a reservation or a contract.

## Enquire from the level you are looking at

Every level in Future has an Enquire button.

| Level | Example | What the team receives |
|---|---|---|
| Complex | District IO | The complex and its figures |
| Phase | Phase 1A | The phase and its figures |
| Sub-phase | Phase 1A-1 | The sub-phase and its buildings |
| Building | Techno Lab Block | The building and its design data |

## Fill in the form

![the enquiry form over the map, with the property figures at the top](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-district-io-1a-form.jpg)

1. Select **Enquire**. The form opens over the map, with the figures of what you selected at the top.
2. Enter your details:
   - Full name
   - Company name
   - Email
   - Phone number
3. Optional: add a message. Useful things to include:
   - the floor area you need
   - the use, for example office, lab or residential
   - when you want to move in
4. Select **Enquire** to send. Your details go to the DSO commercial team.

To continue on your phone instead, scan the QR code in the left panel.
