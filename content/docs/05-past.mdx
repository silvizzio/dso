A buyer who commits off-plan takes a delivery risk: the risk that the building is late, changes, or is not built at all. The best evidence against that risk is what the developer has already built.

Past shows that evidence. DSO HQ was completed in 2005, TechnoHub 4 in 2015 and LIU 8 in 2020. Step through the archived satellite imagery and see the district fill in around them.

![the district in 2020: archived satellite imagery and the three buildings completed by that year](/dso/api/img/05-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

## Three buildings were delivered between 2005 and 2020

| Year | Property | What it added |
|---|---|---|
| 2005 | DSO HQ | The headquarters of the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority |
| 2015 | TechnoHub 4 | Campus offices and labs |
| 2020 | LIU 8 | Light industrial units |

The three buildings are of three different kinds: a headquarters, a campus of offices and labs, and light industrial units. DSO has built each type and runs it today. District IO combines the first two at a larger scale, with homes and a hotel added.

## The archive shows the site as it was

The map switches to archived satellite images for the year you choose. These are photographs of the site at that time, not renderings. Step from 2010 to 2026 and watch roads, plots and buildings appear.

| Region | What changes |
|---|---|
| Map | Archived satellite imagery for the selected year |
| Left panel | Only the areas that existed in that year, each with its completion year |
| Right panel | The Archive view: description, buildings over time, development records |

The development records in the right panel list what was completed in each year, and mark the most recent addition as new. Read together with the imagery, they show both what DSO built and the district that grew around it.

## Past has no live data

Past has no CCTV, no building data and no leasing status. None of these exist for an earlier year. District IO does not show in Past, because no part of it existed before today.
