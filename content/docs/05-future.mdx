Future shows the district as planned. It is the default view for sales, because District IO is the main offer.

<Callout type="warning" title="Projected figures">
All figures in Future are projected. The areas come from the DSO HQ Concept Design Stage Report. The report gives phases, not calendar dates. The delivery years on screen are indicative until DIEZ confirms the programme.
</Callout>

## Future overview

![Future: District IO in the district, with the areas of interest and their status](/dso/api/img/05-future-lod3.jpg)

## District IO phases

Select District IO to see its phases. The left panel shows the delivery track at the top and the list of phases below it.

![District IO: the delivery track, the five phases and their markers on the map](/dso/api/img/05-future-lod3-district-io.jpg)

The delivery track has four steps: Concept, Start, Complete and Handover.

The report splits District IO into five phases. The areas below are gross floor area (GFA) in m².

| Phase | GFA | What it contains |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | 194,707 | Office and techno lab, all the residential (704 homes), retail, open space |
| 1B | 123,807 | Office and techno lab, retail, open space |
| 1C | 128,987 | Office and techno lab, the largest Phase 1 retail, open space |
| 1D | 82,980 | Office and techno lab, retail, open space |
| 2 | 64,819 | Hotel (440 keys), convention centre, museum, retail, open space |
| Total | 595,300 | |

## Timeline at 2030

Move the timeline to a year to see which phases exist in that year. Phases that are not built yet in that year are shown as planned.
