Building Info opens live data from the building management system (BMS), the software that runs cooling, lighting and power. It answers questions a brochure cannot:

- Is the building comfortable?
- Is it well maintained?
- What will it cost to run?

For a tenant choosing between buildings, this is evidence, not a claim.

![TechnoHub 4 building level: system status, comfort, and occupancy, temperature and energy load by floor](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4-building-level.jpg)

## Read the headline figures

| Item | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Systems online | How many building systems report data. A high figure means the building is monitored and maintained. |
| Zone comfort | How many zones are in the comfort range for temperature and air quality |
| Retail | How much of the retail space is let |
| Set point | The temperature the cooling aims for |
| Load now | The current power draw |
| Energy today | Energy use today against a typical day |

## Compare the floors

The charts under By level show three measures for each floor: occupancy, temperature and energy load.

1. Read each bar as one floor, from the ground floor (G) at the left to the top floor at the right.
2. Compare the floors against each other, not against a target.
3. Look for a floor that stands out:
   - **much warmer** than the floors around it: ask about cooling on that floor
   - **much more energy**: ask what drives the load
   - **low occupancy**: this may be where space is available

<Callout type="note" title="Indicative data">
Building data on this screen is indicative until DSO connects the building management system.
</Callout>
