A prospective tenant may want to know how a building is managed as well as what the space looks like. Cooling, power use and occupancy can raise questions about comfort and running costs that a property description alone will not answer.

**Building Info** is intended to present selected readings from the building management system (BMS), which controls systems such as cooling, lighting and power. The prototype combines those indicators with floor areas and uses; its operating figures remain indicative until DSO connects the BMS.

![TechnoHub 4: floor uses and areas, with an occupancy comparison below](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4-building-level.jpg)

## Opening Building Info

Select **Building Info** from a property where the action is available. It is not limited to space offered for lease: the LIU 8 example below shows the same type of information for a leased building.

![LIU 8 Building Info: system readings, use mix and floor details](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-liu-8-2.jpg)

The indicators at the top summarise system monitoring, comfort conditions and current power use. Below them, the use mix shows how the floor area is allocated, followed by a list of floors from the highest level down to **G**. Each row shows the use and area of that floor. The TechnoHub 4 example also includes an occupancy chart for comparing floors.

## Interpreting the figures

Use the readings to prepare questions rather than to draw conclusions about the building from a single number. A difference between floors may reflect how each space is used, and the team may need to explain the circumstances behind it.

Occupancy and leasing availability are separate measures. A low occupancy figure does not establish that a floor is available to rent; check the unit list and confirm the leasing status with the team.

Once connected, this information can help visitors and the DSO team discuss how the building operates using the same figures. It presents a limited set of indicators for a property conversation, while detailed investigation remains with the facilities team.
