Future shows District IO, the planned DSO HQ Complex, as it will be when built. It is the default state of the kiosk, because District IO is the main offer. Everything in Future is off-plan: you judge the complex by its figures, its phases and its design, not by a building you can visit.

![Future: District IO in the district, with the areas of interest and their status](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3.jpg)

<Callout type="warning" title="Projected figures">
All figures in Future are projected. The areas come from the DSO HQ Concept Design Stage Report. The report gives phases, not calendar dates. Delivery years on screen are indicative until DIEZ confirms the programme.
</Callout>

## District IO at a glance

- **595,300 m² of floor area** in five phases, on a 164,420 m² site.
- **A business district first.** Office and techno lab space is 69% of the floor area.
- **704 homes**, all in Phase 1A, in four residential towers.
- **A 440 key hotel**, a convention centre and a museum, all in Phase 2.
- **Concept design stage.** The mix and the phases are set. The programme dates are still for DIEZ to confirm.

## What you can do in Future

1. [Explore District IO](/dso/docs/04-explore-district-io)
   - See how the floor area splits between uses, and which homes it offers.
2. [Compare the Phases](/dso/docs/05-compare-the-phases)
   - See the five phases on the map, compare what each contains, and see which exist in a given year.
3. [Drill Down to a Building](/dso/docs/06-drill-down-to-a-building)
   - Go from a phase to its buildings, and to the floors of one building.
4. [Enquire Off-Plan](/dso/docs/07-enquire-off-plan)
   - Send an enquiry from the complex, a phase, a sub-phase or a building.

## How to read Future

- **Projected, not final.** Areas can move a little as the design develops.
- **Intent, not a floor plan.** The mix and phasing tell you what kind of place it will be. Exact areas come in the lease or sale terms.
- **Years are indicative.** The delivery years on screen are placeholders until the programme is confirmed.
