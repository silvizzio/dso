District IO is DSO's main proposed development and is still at the concept design stage.
Future helps visitors understand the plans by placing the complex in its actual location on the district map.
Existing buildings provide context for what is proposed.

Visitors can explore one phase at a time, move through the planned delivery years and open individual buildings to examine their floors.
Future is the default timeline state, giving District IO the first detailed view in a visit.

## Understanding the proposal in context

A plan or render shows a particular view of a development.
The map lets visitors explore its surroundings as well, including neighbouring buildings and the roads leading to it.

A closer LOD 3 view shows the proposal beside DSO HQ, making the relationship between the existing headquarters and the new complex easier to understand.

![The proposed District IO complex behind DSO HQ at LOD 3](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-context.jpg)

They can start with the whole complex, choose a phase and then look at a building or individual floor.
The delivery timeline adds another consideration: which parts are expected to be ready in the year they hope to move in.
Together, these views help visitors assess both the space and its setting.

## How Future supports an enquiry

Buyers and tenants can use Future to identify the parts of District IO that suit their needs.
They can compare options at their own pace and ask for more information once they have a specific property in mind.

The commercial team receives that selection with the enquiry.
This gives them a place to start the discussion, rather than asking the visitor to describe everything they have just explored.

## Exploring Future

1. [Explore District IO](/dso/docs/04-explore-district-io) to compare the phases and find one that suits your needs.
2. [Step through the delivery years](/dso/docs/05-step-through-the-delivery-years) to see which phases and buildings are expected to be ready in each year.
3. [Drill down to a building](/dso/docs/06-drill-down-to-a-building) to examine its floors and proposed uses.
4. [Enquire off-plan](/dso/docs/07-enquire-off-plan) when you want to discuss a selection with the DSO team.

These tasks follow common questions about a proposed property: where it is, when it will be ready and whether the space is suitable.
Visitors can enquire at any point without completing every step.

## Distinguishing plans from confirmed information

Future presents planned development, so projected figures need to be clearly identified.
Some area figures come from the concept design report, while delivery dates and building-level details remain indicative.
The [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) explains these distinctions.

Each view introduces the details relevant to the next selection, keeping the full complex manageable on screen.
Now and Past remain accessible for visitors who want to compare the proposal with existing buildings or review DSO's completed work.
