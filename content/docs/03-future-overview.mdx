Future introduces District IO, DSO's main proposed development, which is still at the concept design stage. It places the planned complex on the district map so visitors can see its location, surrounding buildings and access roads before looking at the space itself.

This is the default timeline setting. From the district overview, visitors can open a phase, explore its buildings and floors, or compare the years in the proposed delivery programme.

## Seeing where District IO will sit

The map allows visitors to look beyond an individual building render. They can examine the whole complex and its surroundings, then move closer to understand how the proposed buildings relate to each other. The LOD 3 example below shows District IO behind the existing DSO HQ.

![The proposed District IO complex behind DSO HQ at LOD 3](/dso/api/img/03-future-lod3-context.jpg)

For someone considering space here, location and timing matter together. Exploring a phase shows its proposed uses and size; choosing a delivery year helps explain what else is expected to be ready nearby at that point.

## Exploring the proposal

1. [Explore District IO](/dso/docs/04-explore-district-io) to compare the phases by their uses, size and target year.
2. [Step through the delivery years](/dso/docs/05-step-through-the-delivery-years) to see how the complex is expected to develop.
3. [Drill down to a building](/dso/docs/06-drill-down-to-a-building) to examine its proposed floors and uses.
4. [Enquire off-plan](/dso/docs/07-enquire-off-plan) to discuss a phase or building with the DSO team.

There is no need to complete these tasks in order. A visitor can open the enquiry form as soon as they find something they want to discuss. Once submission is connected, the intended process will include the selected property with their contact details so the team can respond to that interest.

## Reading plans as plans

The buildings shown in Future represent the proposal, including when they appear fully built in the model. Some area figures come from the concept design report, but delivery dates and building-level details are still indicative. The [Reference](/dso/docs/17-reference) identifies the sources and what needs confirmation.

Visitors can switch to Now to explore existing properties or Past to review completed projects. These provide examples of DSO's work without changing the proposed status of District IO.
