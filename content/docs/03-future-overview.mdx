District IO is DSO's main new offer, and none of it has been built yet. That means every buyer has to judge it from a plan, and a plan asks them to use their imagination to picture the final result.

Future takes most of that guesswork away. It places District IO on the live map of the district, in its actual location and alongside buildings that are already standing. Visitors can move through the complex phase by phase, watch it grow over time, and look inside individual buildings down to their floors. Future is the default timeline state, because it is where the offer lives.

## Turning a plan into a place visitors can explore

A plan on paper shows a complex from a single angle, at a single moment, and without anything around it. Future changes how a visitor experiences District IO in three important ways.

1. **Location.** The complex sits on the real map, so visitors can see its neighbours, the surrounding roads and how far things are from each other.
2. **Navigation.** Visitors can move from the whole complex to a single phase, then to a building, then to a floor, at their own pace.
3. **Time.** Visitors can watch the complex grow phase by phase and see which parts will exist in any given year.

Together, these turn a static plan into a place that visitors can explore much as they would explore a finished district.

## Future handles the first half of the sales conversation

For buyers and tenants, Future makes the offer feel real. They can find the part of District IO that matches their needs, see what will surround it, and form their own view without an agent standing beside them.

For the DSO commercial team, anyone who reaches the Enquire action in Future has already explored the options, compared them and made a choice. The conversation that follows can start from a specific interest instead of a general question.

## A typical visit through Future

1. [Explore District IO](/dso/docs/04-explore-district-io): break the complex down into its phases and find the part that fits.
2. [Step Through the Delivery Years](/dso/docs/05-step-through-the-delivery-years): move through the delivery years to see which phases and buildings will exist in each one.
3. [Drill Down to a Building](/dso/docs/06-drill-down-to-a-building): go from a phase to a building to a floor, and decide whether it fits.
4. [Enquire Off-Plan](/dso/docs/07-enquire-off-plan): register interest from exactly where the visitor has stopped.

Each step answers the question raised by the one before it. Once visitors find a phase they like, they want to know what will surround it. Once they know that, they want to know what the building itself is like. And once they know that, they are ready to act.

## How Future stays credible

- **Everything is clearly marked as projected.** Future never presents a planned figure as a fact. If a visitor caught even one overstatement, it would cast doubt on every other number on the screen.
- **Detail is revealed step by step.** Each step shows only what the visitor needs for their next decision, so a complex of this size never feels overwhelming.
- **Future comes first, but it is never on its own.** Now and Past are always one tap away, so visitors can check the offer against the present and against DSO's track record whenever they want.
