District IO is DSO's main new offer, and nothing of it has been built.
Every buyer judges it from a plan, and a plan asks the buyer to imagine the result.

Future removes most of that imagining.
It places District IO on the live map of the district, in its real location, next to buildings that already stand.
A visitor can move through it phase by phase, see it grow over time and inspect a single building down to its floors.
Future is the default timeline state, because it holds the offer.

## Future turns a plan into a place a visitor can move through

A plan on paper shows a complex from one angle, at one moment, without its surroundings.
Future changes three things about how a visitor meets District IO.

1. Location.
   The complex sits on the real map, so its neighbours, roads and distances are visible.
2. Navigation.
   The visitor moves from the whole complex to a phase, a building and a floor, at their own pace.
3. Time.
   The visitor sees the complex grow phase by phase, and which parts exist in any given year.

Together, these turn a static plan into something a visitor can explore the way they would explore a finished district.

## Future does the first half of the sales conversation

For buyers and tenants, the offer becomes concrete.
They find the part of District IO that suits their needs, see what will surround it and judge it without an agent present.

For the DSO commercial team, a visitor who reaches the Enquire action in Future has already explored, compared and chosen.
The conversation that follows starts from a specific interest rather than a general question.

## A visit through Future follows four steps

1. [Explore District IO](/dso/docs/04-explore-district-io): break the complex into its phases and find the part that fits.
2. [Compare the Phases](/dso/docs/05-compare-the-phases): use the timeline to see what will surround that part, and when.
3. [Drill Down to a Building](/dso/docs/06-drill-down-to-a-building): go from a phase to a building to a floor, and judge fit.
4. [Enquire Off-Plan](/dso/docs/07-enquire-off-plan): register interest from exactly where the visitor stopped.

Each step answers the question the previous one raises.
Once a visitor finds a phase, they ask what will surround it.
Once they know that, they ask what the building itself is like.
Once they know that, they act.

## Three rules keep Future credible

- Everything is marked as projected.
  Future never presents a planned figure as a fact, because one discovered overstatement would undermine every other figure on screen.
- Detail arrives in steps.
  Each step shows only what the visitor needs for the next choice, so a complex of this size never feels overwhelming.
- Future opens first, but never alone.
  Now and Past sit one tap away, so a visitor can test the offer against the present and the record at any point.
