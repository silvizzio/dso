Move the timeline back and the district rolls back with it. Step from 2010 to 2026 and watch roads, plots and buildings appear.

![the archive view in 2020, with the year steps in the left panel](/dso/api/img/05-past-lod3-2020.jpg)

## Choose a year

1. Open the timeline in the left panel and select **Past**.
2. Select a year step, or use the arrows beside the timeline:
   - 2010
   - 2015
   - 2020
   - 2025
   - 2026
3. Read the map and the panels for that year.

## See what changes on screen

| Region | What changes |
|---|---|
| Map | Archived satellite imagery for the selected year |
| Left panel | Only the areas that existed in that year, each with its completion year |
| Right panel | The Archive view: description, buildings over time, development records |

The archived images are photographs of the site at that time. What you see is what was there.
