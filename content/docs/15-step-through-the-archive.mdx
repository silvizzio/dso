The archive shows DSO at intervals through satellite imagery from earlier years. Selecting a year changes the map to the corresponding image, making it possible to compare where roads, plots and buildings were added.

The photographs record the site at the time they were captured. Read them alongside the property records to connect the changes visible on the map with building names and completion dates.

## Using the archive controls

Switch the timeline to **Past**, then choose an available year with the year steps or arrows. The map changes to that year's archive imagery. Select **View in timelapse** beneath the timeline to watch the sequence, or keep using the year controls to examine a particular period.

The left panel identifies buildings and their completion years, while the right panel presents the district's delivery record. These records are intended to follow the selected year, but later entries may remain visible in a dimmed state. Check each record's date before treating the building as complete in the year you are viewing.

## What belongs in the historical view

Past excludes live cameras, current building data and leasing status because those describe the present. District IO is also absent from the historical maps because it had not been built.

The prototype still contains inconsistencies between some dates and headings. Those need to be reconciled with DSO's records before the map and completion panels can be treated as a consistent historical account.
