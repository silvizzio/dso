## Why the archive uses photographs

Satellite imagery records the site at the time of capture.
It lets visitors examine physical changes across the available years without relying on a reconstructed model of the past.
The accompanying completion record adds the names and dates needed to understand those changes.

## Selecting a year

Switch the timeline to Past, then choose an available year using the year steps or arrows.
The map changes to the corresponding archive imagery.
Select **View in timelapse** beneath the timeline to follow the district's growth as a sequence, or use the individual year steps to examine a particular view.

The left panel lists the buildings that existed at that time, with their completion years.
The right panel shows the district's delivery record.
Move through the available years to compare how the district developed.

## Keeping the map and records together

The map and completion highlights are intended to follow the selected timeline year.
Later records may remain visible as dimmed entries, so read their dates before treating them as completed in the selected year.
The prototype still contains date and label inconsistencies that need checking against DSO records.

Live cameras, current building data and leasing status are excluded because they describe the present.
District IO is also absent from these historical views because it had not been built.
