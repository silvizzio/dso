Building Info opens live data from the building management system (BMS), the software that runs cooling, lighting and power. It answers questions a brochure cannot: is the building comfortable, is it well maintained, and what will it cost to run? For a tenant choosing between buildings, this is evidence, not a claim.

![TechnoHub 4 building level: system status, comfort, and occupancy, temperature and energy load by floor](/dso/api/img/04-now-lod3-available-technohub-4-building-level.jpg)

## Read the headline figures

| Item | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Systems online | How many building systems report data. A high figure means the building is monitored and maintained. |
| Zone comfort | How many zones are in the comfort range for temperature and air quality |
| Retail, set point, load now | Retail space let, the temperature the cooling aims for, and the current power draw |
| Energy today | Energy use today against a typical day |

## Compare the floors

The charts under By level show occupancy, temperature and energy load for each floor. Each bar is one floor, from the ground floor (G) at the left to the top floor at the right.

Read the floors against each other rather than against a target. A floor that is much warmer, or uses much more energy than the floors around it, is worth asking about. A floor with low occupancy may be where space is available.

<Callout type="note" title="Indicative data">
Building data on this screen is indicative until DSO connects the building management system.
